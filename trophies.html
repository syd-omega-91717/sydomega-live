ACHIEVEMENT VAULT ARCHITECTURE
TWELVE TROPHIES
12 sovereign trophies span the complete element spectrum: Genesis Mark (Sand), Knowledge Crown (Wind), Mastery Seal (Fire), Contribution Sigil (Water), Elemental Badge (Sand), Celestial Mark (Wind), Sovereign Crest (Sand), Bloodline Trophy (Metal), Economic Seal (Fire), Oracle Crown (Water), Omega Guardian (Fire), and Absolute Apex (Sand).
TWELVE MEDALS
Order medals recognize domain mastery: First Light (Genesis), Knowledge Pioneer (Academy), Master of Craft (Mastery), Economic Builder (Economy), Celestial Navigator (Cosmos), Order Architect (Platform), Family Guardian (Lineage), Consultation Master (Services), Research Pioneer (Intel), Prediction Oracle (Forecast), Heritage Keeper (History), Omega Sovereign (Apex).
TWELVE CERTIFICATES
Certificates are issued upon completion of sovereign study paths. They range from Genesis Certificate (Platform Foundation) through Knowledge Foundation, Mastery Levels I and II, Economic Analyst, Celestial Navigator, Sovereign Architect, Heritage Scholar, Oracle Prophet, Order Consultant, Platform Master, to Omega Sovereign (Absolute Apex — All Domains).
ACHIEVEMENT ENGINE
Achievements are stored in three Supabase tables: trophies, medals, and certificates — each linked by user_id. Platform owners hold all 36 achievements by definition. Member achievements are earned through axis progression, task completion, and sovereign domain mastery. The Omega Sovereign achievement requires all axes at maximum.
