### Studios Worlds layer: sourced thrones, places, pantheons, sky and planets (2026-11-07)

**Why.** The Studios canon had characters and franchises but no kings, cities, pantheons, sky or planets, and `docs/studios/OMEGA_STUDIOS_BASELINE_AUDIT.md` proposed a new `/worlds.html`. A first draft of this layer guessed the twelve Zodiac worlds. Reading `docs/canon/zodiac-wars/BIBLE.md` section 3 showed that draft would have contradicted merged canon (Athena rules Ceres, Demeter sits on Pluto, Hestia on Saturn). Tracing the "Light rulers of Mars, Juno, Neptune" claim in `PACKAGE.md` found that `grep -c Juno media.html omega-agents.json` is 0 and 0: Juno is Hera's Roman name in `pantheons.html`, not a world named by any platform file. Only `media.html` fixes Mars, Ceres and Neptune ("Ares presses Mars... Athena presses Ceres... Poseidon presses Neptune").

**Fix.** `config/omega-studio-worlds.json` (13 worlds, 15 places, 6 thrones, 5 pantheons, 4 sky events, 6 open questions), each entry with a status and cited files; `scripts/studio-worlds-check.py`; `worlds.html` (5 tabs, status filter, connection links, SVG sky wheel and orrery, opt-in orbit motion off under reduced motion), wired in `nav.js`. Registry `hera` role corrected from "Light ruler of Libra and Juno" to "...(her world, Juno, is only proposed)"; bibles regenerated. Undecided slots (Empire capital, Kingdom of Ash ruler, the Aurelith's world) are OPEN, not invented.

**Verified.** `python3 scripts/studio-worlds-check.py` -> OK (13 worlds, 15 places, 6 thrones, 5 pantheons). `python3 -m unittest scripts.tests.test_studio_worlds` -> 24 tests, OK; they plant 20+ violations (invented source, guessed world promoted to REPO_CANON from a proposal, real-world king, stale character table, unraised OPEN slot) and each is caught. Headless Chromium via the verify-in-browser harness at 1280 and 375 wide: 5 tabs, 6/15/5/4+12/13+13 cards and nodes, filter, detail and jump links, canon-module signs, orbit animation moves Venus, no duplicate ids, 0 px overflow, no page errors.

**Not done / not verified.** No human has used the page; no real-hardware performance. The nine proposed worlds, the Shadows and the thirteenth seat are the assistant's proposals, not approved. Which world the Seven Stones places stand on is undecided. `config/omega-studio-worlds.json` ships publicly (a page fetches it), and its notes contain unreleased plot of the approved film; the registry is deliberately not shipped. The remaining 8 proposed Studios routes, 24 screenplays, about 150 episode scripts, 3 game design documents, `studio_*` schema and rights records are untouched. `PACKAGE.md` for The Rise of Aries still says "Light rulers of Mars, Juno, Neptune" citing `omega-agents.json`; left as written, flagged here.
