TREASURY SCIENCE
01
SOVEREIGN WEALTH DOCTRINE
Sovereign wealth funds (Norway, Singapore, UAE) hold assets in perpetuity across generations. The personal sovereign applies the same principle: categorize wealth by time horizon — consumption, generation, legacy.
02
RESERVE RATIOS
Classic personal finance recommends 3-6 months of expenses in liquid reserves. The sovereign standard is 12 months emergency + 10-20% of net worth in opportunity capital. Liquidity is optionality.
03
WEALTH TRILEMMA
All wealth faces a trilemma: liquidity, return, safety — pick two. Cash is liquid and safe but low-return. Real estate is illiquid, safe, moderate-return. Crypto is liquid, high-return, high-risk.
04
CASH FLOW SOVEREIGNTY
Kiyosaki's framework: assets produce income, liabilities consume it. The treasury goal is a system where passive cash flows exceed expenses — complete operational sovereignty without active labor income.
05
DEPRECIATION AWARENESS
All assets depreciate except those that compound: equity stakes, intellectual property, skilled human capital. Track cost basis against current value to identify wealth creation vs. wealth preservation assets.
06
THE WEALTH EQUATION
Net Worth = Assets − Liabilities. But sovereign wealth adds time-adjusted present value: what is each asset's real worth discounted for illiquidity, tax drag, and concentration risk? True wealth is net of friction.
