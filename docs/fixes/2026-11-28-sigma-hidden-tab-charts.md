### sigma.html: four charts drawn at a made-up width while hidden, then stretched up to 3.9x (2026-11-28)

**How it was found.** A second canvas sweep that covers what the first one could not see: for the 143 pages with a canvas it records which canvases are hidden at load (929), clicks every tab-like control one by one, and re-measures. A canvas is reported only if it was hidden at load, became visible, and has painted pixels (so an undrawn default store is not a finding). 230 became visible and drawn: 96 were fine, 121 were a uniform 2x store on a dpr-3 screen (the repo's deliberate cap, not a stretch), and 13 were distorted, on three pages: `sigma.html` (4), `queue.html` (1), `series.html` (8). This entry fixes `sigma.html`, the worst.

**Why.** `sigma-axes`, `sigma-auth-curve`, `sigma-elem-matrix` and `sigma-scatter` live in tabs that are hidden at load. Each was drawn once with `var W = cv.offsetWidth || <fallback>` (200 / 400 / 500 / 500), `cv.width = W` (1x, no device-pixel-ratio), and no observer. Measured on `main` at `b25abf04f`: `sigma-axes` stored 104 px wide and shown 403 px (3.9x sideways), `sigma-auth-curve` 313 shown 849, `sigma-elem-matrix` and `sigma-scatter` 233 shown 862. In the render the "AXIS A/B/C" labels are smeared and blurry, the radar triangle is flattened wide, and its side labels are cut off. The fifth chart on the page, `drawDist`, already had the correct pattern (CLAUDE.md 8.1 class 3); these four did not.

**Fix.** `sigma.html` only. One local helper `fitBox(cv, H, redraw)` measures the canvas's own box, returns nothing while that box is 0 (so nothing is drawn at a made-up width), draws at the device pixel ratio (capped at 3), and installs one `ResizeObserver` that repaints when the box gets a width or changes it. The four charts call it. `drawAuthCurve` takes arguments, so it stores the latest ones on the canvas and the observer redraws with those, not the first call's. **A second change is needed because of the first:** the radar's radius was `min(W,H)*0.4`. At the old stretched 104 px width the radius was width-limited and hid a latent bug; drawn at the real width it is height-limited (H = 140), and the top label ("A: ...", placed r+16 out, 9 px tall) is clipped at the canvas's top edge (painted top at 0 px). The radius is now `min(W,H)*0.32` (painted top 5 px, bottom 103.5 of 140, with margin at 1000 px and 390 px wide).

**Verified.** `node scripts/verify-sigma-canvases.js`: PASSED 14 of 14 at 1000@1, 1000@3 and 390@3 (all four charts become visible and drawn after opening the tabs; each store is its box x the pixel ratio on both axes; after a resize each visible chart is repainted at its new width; the radar labels are not clipped by the canvas edge; no page errors). The same script against the pre-fix `main`: 3 failures (`sigma-axes` 104x140 store in a 403x140 box). With the first attempt at the fix (radius factor 0.4) the new clipping check fails 3 of 14, which is how the second change was found and why it is pinned. Before and after screenshots of `sigma-axes` and `sigma-auth-curve` at 1000 px, ratio 2, were compared by eye; the clipped-label bound was then measured numerically, because reading pixel positions off a scaled screenshot proved unreliable. `python3 -m unittest scripts.tests.test_sigma_canvases`: OK; the browser case passes with `OMEGA_SIGMA_BROWSER=1`.
