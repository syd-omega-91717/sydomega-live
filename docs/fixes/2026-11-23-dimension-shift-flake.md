### The dimension-shift test flake: assert which seats answer, not canvas brightness (2026-11-23)

**Why.** `scripts/verify-dimension-shift.js` check "the council: the matching executors brighten" failed intermittently on a correct build. Measured on pristine `origin/main` in a worktree (so it is not a regression from anything newer): 20/21, 21/21, 20/21 in three runs (2 of 3 failing), and 2 of 3 failing on the branch too, i.e. identical. It compared the *maximum total coin-face pixel mass* of a VOID shift (no executor has VOID) against a FIRE shift, taken in two separate runs of a rotating scene: 5.15 vs 5.48 in the failing run, against a 1.15x threshold. A paired prototype (6 alternating VOID/FIRE shifts, each against its own un-shifted baseline) confirmed the metric cannot separate the cases: the *baseline with no shift at all* moved between about 2.0 and 2.8, VOID peaks reached 4.27 and FIRE peaks fell to 3.02 (CLAUDE.md 8.4 already records this scene swinging about 40 percent on total light energy). No pixel threshold could be honest here.

**Fix.** (1) `omega-sculpture.js`: the council scene now publishes the seats answering the current shift as `scene.answering` (rewritten every frame, `answering.push(k)` only where the existing `answer` is non-zero) and `OmegaSculpture.status()` hands out a copy. Read-only; no behaviour change. (2) `verify-dimension-shift.js` asserts the cause: a Fire shift makes exactly seats 0, 4, 8 answer (Sentinel, Sovereign, Beacon, computed from `omega-agents.json`), a Water shift exactly 3, 7, 11, a VOID shift none across the whole window, the pulse clears by itself, and nothing answers under reduced motion. (3) The rest of the file's fixed sleeps, which failed only under load, became causal waits: the gap past the 2.5 s throttle is measured from the last shift that actually happened (`detail.at`); label and wash are snapshotted by a `MutationObserver` at the moment of insertion (the label lives 1.7 s; a later read on a starved machine finds it gone); cleanup waits for the elements to be removed; the council retries a shift up to 5 times because a starved frame loop can skip the whole 1.9 s pulse window, which is the product behaving correctly; `trigger()` is retried until it returns true instead of sleeping past the throttle; the burst is dispatched in one tick (three real saves can settle more than 2.5 s apart under load and then are, correctly, separate shifts). Section 1 still covers the real fetch chain.

**Verified.** Alone: 27/27, repeatedly. Under load (6 copies at once, which broke the old version): first rewrite 24 runs: 23 clean, 1 failure (the burst check's timing premise, fixed afterwards); that fix was then confirmed alone (27/27) but the 6-way stress was not repeated after it. Findings along the way, all fixed: my first council check triggered the Water shift 2.4 s after the Fire one, inside the 2.5 s throttle, so it was silently ignored (a race I introduced; the retry-until-fired helper replaced it); the first rewrite's "ended 2.3 s later" read was a fixed sleep too. **Mutation-tested against the scene and the module**: element match inverted -> Fire and Water checks fail; seats never answer -> both fail; the pulse never clears (`left` stays 1) -> "clears by itself" and Water fail; the module's throttle removed -> the burst and habits checks fail. `test_dimension_shift.py`/`test_council_scene.py`/`test_dashboard_tabs.py` 44 OK. **Not caught by a browser run, covered statically**: ignoring reduced motion in the council's `answer` expression passes the browser suite, because under reduced motion the scene renders one still frame and never runs the update loop; `test_the_council_listens_and_answers_only_matching_elements` pins `(!reduceMotion && left > 0 &&` and a new contract pins the published `answering` state. A first M3 mutation (`until > 0`) was inert (it makes `left` negative so nothing answers); redone as `left = 1`.
