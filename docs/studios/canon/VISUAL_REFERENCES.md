# Visual references (mood only) — not assets

Nine images were supplied as direction. They are **third-party works from commercial and stock sites** and are not
licensed for this repository: none is copied, embedded, traced or served. Production art is to be original, derived
from the written direction below. Two of the images show recognisable commercial games (a PlayStation store page for
*Dragon Age: The Veilguard* and a strategy-game screenshot), so designs must not imitate their distinctive characters,
silhouettes or logos.

| Reference (source site) | What it shows | Direction taken | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Violet crystal over ruins (zazencodes.com) | A lone figure before a floating faceted crystal, lattice of light lines, gothic ruins | A stone as a lattice node: faceted, violet-white, wired into the sky by thin geodesic lines | Myth of the Stones, the Seven Stones set pieces |
| Anime armoured ensemble with a golden sigil (story.com) | Stylised anime knights, dragons, a gold wheel | **Style conflict.** The brief asks for cinematic realism; this is anime. Kept only as an ensemble-poster composition idea | Zodiac Wars poster layout |
| Golden megacity from above (caelora-corporation.com) | Warm-lit towers joined by looping rails under a nebula | Amber city light against deep blue; ring transit loops | The Omega Chronicles, Last Galactic Empire capital |
| Ancient portal between giant statues (wallpaperswide.com) | Cold blue ruin, two guardian statues, a glowing orb, tiny figure for scale | Scale by a single human figure; guardians as architecture | The Lost Civilizations |
| Fleet battle in a nebula (ageofspace.net) | Capital ship, coloured laser fire | Coloured beam language per faction | Last Galactic Empire |
| Gothic ruins with floating structures (store.playstation.com) | Moody blue dusk, a hooded colossus statue | Floating citadels as set dressing; not copied | Myth of the Stones: Origins, environments |

Palette to carry across: Omega gold `#C9A84C`, cyan `#00E5FF`, void `#0A0A0F` (repository tokens), with violet as the
Myth accent. Contrast and reduced-motion rules from `CLAUDE.md` §4 apply to any in-page use.
