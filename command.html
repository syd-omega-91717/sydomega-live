THE SCIENCE OF DAILY COMMAND
PREFRONTAL CORTEX DEPLETION — Decision-making quality degrades ~2% per decision. Batch trivial decisions in morning routines. Reserve peak prefrontal bandwidth (7–11am) for strategic priorities.

IMPLEMENTATION INTENTIONS — Research shows "I will do X at time Y in location Z" increases follow-through by 91% vs vague intentions. The command brief forces this specificity.

ULTRADIAN RHYTHMS — The brain cycles through 90-minute rest-activity rhythms. Schedule deep work in 90-min blocks. Use natural dip (1–3pm) for admin, communication, and light tasks.

PLANNING STREAK EFFECT — Consistent daily planning creates meta-cognitive loops that increase self-awareness, reduce reactive behavior, and build sovereign identity over time.
