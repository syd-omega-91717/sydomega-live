ELEMENT TRIBE SYSTEM — SOVEREIGN SCIENCE
HOW TRIBES WORK

Each sovereign is born into one of the nine elemental tribes based on their assigned element (determined by birth sign or authority unlock level). Tribes are not chosen — they are discovered. The platform tracks tribe membership, authority distribution, and inter-tribal resonance in real time.

The Tribal Dominance Chart reflects the live count of members per tribe. The Affinity Matrix shows the conceptual resonance coefficient between any two elements — a measure of how naturally those elemental forces amplify or challenge each other.
