### A "not financial advice" notice on every money page (omega-finance-notice.js) (2026-11-23)

**Why.** The owner's compliance brief (section E) asks for "clear disclaimers separating entertainment/simulation from regulated financial or investment advice" before any live token or paid tier. A grep of `investment.html`, `blockchain.html`, `wallet.html`, `marketplace.html` and `treasury.html` for advice / disclaim / simulat / illustrat / hypothetical returned **0 hits on every one**; `terms.html:86` carries "not advice" for horoscopes only; the single repo hit for "financial advice" was a doc (`docs/OMEGA_SOVEREIGN_PRODUCTION_AND_VALUE_STANDARD.md`). The pages are real: `investment.html` is a portfolio tracker whose data is stored only in the browser, `blockchain.html`/`wallet.html`/`treasury.html` talk about assets and tokens.

**Fix.** `omega-finance-notice.js` (new, ~4 KB, pure ASCII, no network): one `<aside role="note">` at the top of the page's content column reading "NOT FINANCIAL ADVICE. SYD Omega is a personal platform. It is not a bank, broker, exchange or regulated financial service, and nothing on this page is financial, investment, tax or legal advice or an offer to buy or sell anything. Scenarios, projections and scores are illustrations, not predictions or promises of return. Decisions about money are yours; speak to a qualified adviser first." Opt-in by one `<script src="/omega-finance-notice.js" defer>` per page, on 14 pages: the nav.js "invest" section (budget, expenses, invest, investment, portfolio, revenue, treasury, wallet, wealth) plus blockchain, marketplace, income, subscriptions, payments (the money pages `commerce-contract.py` names). `bg.js` untouched; the module holds no page list. **Deliberately narrow wording**: it says what the platform is not and that scenarios are illustrations; it does not say a feature is live, dormant, simulated or real, names no jurisdiction and states no figure, because those change and a notice wrong in one direction is worse than none. Not dismissible (no button, no storage). English only on purpose (no `data-i18n` key, so no pack can word it differently). Placement avoids the CLAUDE.md §4 trap: if `#app`/`main` is the flex-row shell it uses the column after the sidebar instead.

**Verified.** `node scripts/verify-finance-notice.js` 114/114 (each of the 14 pages, signed in via the harness stub, at 1280 and 375 px, plus a negative control and a shell fixture): exactly one notice, visible, in the content column and before the page's first heading, to the right of the sidebar and wider than a sliver, the module's exact words, nothing covering it, nothing interactive in it, inside the viewport at 375. `scripts/tests/test_finance_notice.py` (12 static contracts + the browser run) derives the required page set from nav.js's own page-to-section map, so a new "invest" page without the tag fails. **Mutation-tested**: notice hidden -> fails; appended below the heading -> fails; dismiss button -> fails (browser and static); asserting "simulated" in the text -> fails; a money page losing its tag -> fails; the shell used as the column -> fails **only** on the synthetic fixture (all 14 real pages passed with that mutation, i.e. the guard is exercised by no real page, so the fixture is what proves it). Found by looking, not by a check: other modules insert a hero and a workspace rail above the notice, so "first child" was the wrong assertion (replaced by "before the first heading"); `invest.html` has its own 8 px offset at 375 px that pushed the notice 8 px past the edge (fixed with a `max-width:min(100%,calc(100vw - 16px))`); `income.html` is owner-only and bounces the stub member, so its test runs with an owner profile; `wallet.html` throws `drawAllocCanvas is not defined` on `origin/main` already (not caused here; filed in `GAP_ANALYSIS.md` §S).
