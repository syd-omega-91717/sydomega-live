### Winged lion as a 3-D scene (SCENES.lion) (2026-11-23)

**Why.** The brand's centrepiece, the winged lion over the Omega, existed only as flat art (a painted concept medallion and, since PR #1535, the animated SVG seal). The platform's real-time layer (`omega-sculpture.js`, one shared WebGL context) rendered the Omega signet but not the lion.

**Fix.** `SCENES.lion` in `omega-sculpture.js`, mounted with `data-omega-sculpture="lion"`: a faceted, flat-shaded gold head with a two-ring spiked mane, glowing cyan eyes, six blade feathers per wing (the left wing is the right wing mirrored) beating in a wave from shoulder to tip, nine stars on an arc overhead, and the existing extruded Omega signet beneath. The cursor turns the head and the camera; reduced motion draws one still frame; a narrow mount pulls the camera back so both wings stay in frame. With WebGL absent, `fallback()` draws the flat seal artwork instead of a dead box. Mounted on `sculpture.html` as its own featured stage. Added to the file's mount list and `SCULPT_LABEL` (accessible name). No other scene was touched. It is an illustration: it claims, awards and shows no figure.

**Found by looking, not by assertion.** The first render drew a lion head, threw nothing, and appeared to have no wings; a debug swap to a bright double-sided material showed the wings were there, camouflaged inside the mane's silhouette in the same gold and the same length. That was a design flaw, not culling (re-introducing front-face culling did NOT break the final test suite, which is how I know the culling theory was wrong). Fixed by shrinking the mane (reach 1.2) and making the wings long, fanned and longest at the centre (2.1), and by giving the signet an emissive glow because its lower half fell into black beneath a lion. Also double-sided the feathers anyway (the mirrored wing flips winding). A temporary debug handle was added and removed; a static test pins that no debug residue ships.

**Verified.** `node scripts/verify-lion-scene.js` -> 11/11 in headless Chromium with software WebGL, reading canvas pixels: gold at the head, gold well outside the head on BOTH sides, the picture changes over half a wing beat, one still frame under reduced motion, both wings inside the canvas at 375 px with no overflow, the no-WebGL fallback draws the seal, 0 page errors. Mutation-tested so the suite is known to fail when it should: wings never attached -> 8/11 (left wing, right wing, mobile fail); wings shortened to sit inside the mane -> 8/11 (the same three). `scripts/tests/test_lion_scene.py` 9 tests.

**Not done / not verified.** (1) Rendered with SwiftShader (software), so frame rate on real GPUs and phones is unmeasured; the scene is about 80 meshes. (2) Mounted on `sculpture.html` only, deliberately not on `dashboard.html`: that page is the most used and the owner has not seen the lion yet. (3) The head's lighting still shows a bright patch on one cheek and a dim eye on the shadow side; lit by two point lights, not an environment map. (4) The Omega is the platform's own signet mark (a horseshoe with feet), not the full-legged Omega drawn on the seal. (5) Not deployed.
