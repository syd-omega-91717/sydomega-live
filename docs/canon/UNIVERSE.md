## Decisions

1. **One universe.** The 12-chapter saga on `media.html` is the master chronology. The seven franchises are windows onto it, not rivals.
2. **Zodiac Flux / Zodiac Wars.** "Zodiac Flux" is the in-universe name of the war's opening; "Zodiac Wars" is the franchise title.
3. **`omega-agents.json` stays canonical** for sign, element and god. The April spec's pantheon names (Geb, Enki, Vayu, Thoth) belong to the Cave of the Shadows gate-guardian layer, not to the twelve Houses.
4. **Lore layers, not franchises:** Cave of the Shadows, Babylon Singularity, Solomon's Sovereignty, AI Sovereign.
5. **Matrix = 104,976** (12×12×9×9×9). 729 is the inner cube at each intersection.
