# Ω ARCHIVE Workspace Consolidation

`/archive.html` is the canonical discovery surface for heritage, identity records, KYC, credentials, charter, sigil and membership.

## Specialist routes retained

- `/heritage.html` — historical/cultural archive
- `/bloodline.html` — lineage presentation
- `/character.html` — character identity
- `/identity.html` — identity records
- `/passport.html` — passport presentation
- `/kyc.html` — KYC workflow
- `/credentials.html` — governed credential lifecycle and verification
- `/charter.html` — charter material
- `/sigil.html` — sigil identity surface
- `/membership.html` — membership state

## Trust boundary

The workspace is discovery only. It does not issue, revoke, approve or verify credentials, KYC, membership or identity status. Existing RLS, owner checks, AAL2 gates and governed RPCs remain authoritative.

Historical and lore material must remain distinct from verified records.

## Migration rule

Specialist pages remain deep-link targets until their capabilities are absorbed with capability parity, authorization verification, analytics continuity and production verification.
