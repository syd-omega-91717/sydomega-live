POINTS ARCHITECTURE
THREE AXES
Axis A (Knowledge) — earned through academy, exams, and research. Axis B (Mastery) — earned through gaming, challenges, and certifications. Axis C (Contribution) — earned through publishing, events, and service. Each axis ranges from 0.001 (genesis) to 9.000 (apex).
AUTHORITY FORMULA
AUTH = √(A³+B³+C³) × φ/e where φ = 1.6180339887 (golden ratio) and e = 2.7182818285 (Euler's number). The cube term ensures non-linear growth — advancing from 8 to 9 on any axis yields a larger AUTH gain than advancing from 1 to 2.
TASK COMPLETIONS
Every verified action is logged to the task_completions table with axis_type (knowledge/mastery/contribution), points_earned, and task_name. The log provides a chronological record of all sovereign point gains across the platform lifecycle.
LATTICE POSITION
The 104,976-node lattice (12×12×9×9×9) encodes every possible sovereign coordinate. Your node position is computed from matrix_track, matrix_phase, and ceiling values of axes A, B, and C. The radar diagram shows your triadic position on a normalized A/B/C triangle.
