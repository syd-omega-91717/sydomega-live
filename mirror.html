◈ JOURNALING RESEARCH
Expressive writing for 15-20 minutes, 3-4 days per week, has been shown to improve working memory, reduce intrusive thoughts, and increase psychological wellbeing (Pennebaker & Smyth, 2016). The sovereign check-in compresses this into a structured daily signal under 5 minutes, targeting consistency over duration.
◕ ENERGY-FOCUS CORRELATION
Research on cognitive performance (Kleitman, 1963; Peretz & Bhattacharya, 2020) identifies ultradian rhythms of roughly 90-120 minute cycles. Tracking energy and focus across 30+ days reveals personal performance windows — when your sovereign clarity peak occurs, and how to schedule high-leverage work accordingly.
⬡ MOOD STATES MODEL
The five sovereign mood states map to a valence-arousal model:

TURBULENT — high arousal, negative valence
SUBDUED — low arousal, negative valence
NEUTRAL — baseline state
FOCUSED — moderate arousal, positive valence
SOVEREIGN — high arousal, positive valence (flow-adjacent)

Dominant mood tracking over 7 days identifies chronic state tendencies.
◆ PATTERN RECOGNITION
Minimum 7 entries required for meaningful pattern detection. At 30 entries, energy-focus correlation coefficient becomes statistically stable (r ≥ 0.4 indicates a meaningful link between the two). Tag frequency analysis over 30 days reveals which sovereign forces — DISCIPLINE, FLOW, SHADOW — dominate your experience window. The sovereign who sees the pattern can change it.
