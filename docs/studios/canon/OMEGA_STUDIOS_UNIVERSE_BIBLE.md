## The foundational mythology

Existence rests on three principles: **Matter** (the physical foundation), **Energy** (the force of transformation) and
**Consciousness** (the capacity to understand, influence and create). An ancient event, the **First Convergence**, lets many
civilizations and dimensions emerge. Each age finds a fragment of it and misreads it: as gods, as magic, as technology. The
central mystery is that all of them met the same phenomenon.

The repository already has a nine-element canon (Fire, Water, Wind, Metal, Sand, Soul, Space, Void, The All). The proposed
reconciliation (C-08): the three principles are domains of the nine elements, and Space, Void and The All are the conditions
that frame them.

## Seven divisions

Mythic, Celestial, Omega, Historical, Galactic, Human and Gaming. Every franchise sits in one division and may reach into others
only through a recorded crossover.

## Standing rules

1. Every character, event, artifact, location and storyline gets a stable id in the registry before it is written about.
2. No name token may be shared by two characters unless a conflict records why.
3. A production is not called finished until its media or build exists and publication is verified.
4. Crossovers are earned by the stories, not forced.
