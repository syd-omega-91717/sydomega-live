SLEEP CYCLES

Each cycle lasts ~90 minutes: NREM1 → NREM2 → NREM3 (deep) → REM. For peak restoration, wake at a cycle boundary — use multiples of 90 min from sleep onset (plus ~15 min to fall asleep).

ADENOSINE & CAFFEINE

Adenosine accumulates during wakefulness, cleared during sleep. Caffeine blocks adenosine receptors. Stop caffeine 8–10h before bed. A coffee nap (20 min post-caffeine) lets caffeine absorb while adenosine clears slightly.

LIGHT EXPOSURE

Morning sunlight within 60 min of wake sets your circadian clock and triggers the cortisol peak. Avoid bright blue light 2–3h before bed. Red/amber light minimally suppresses melatonin.

TEMPERATURE PROTOCOL

Core body temperature must drop ~1–3°F for sleep onset. Keep room at 65–68°F (18–20°C). A warm bath 1–2h before bed paradoxically cools the core via vasodilation.

WHY SLEEP IS THE SOVEREIGN'S ULTIMATE WEAPON

Sleep is the single most effective performance reset available. The glymphatic system flushes the brain of metabolic waste (including Alzheimer's precursors) during sleep. Memory consolidates from hippocampus to cortex. Testosterone peaks during deep NREM. Growth hormone is released almost exclusively during slow-wave sleep.

SLEEP STAGES

NREM1 (Light, ~5%): Transition. NREM2 (Baseline, ~45%): Sleep spindles consolidate motor skills. NREM3 (Deep SWS, ~25%): Physical restoration, immune function, emotional processing — dominant in first half. REM (~25%): Dreaming, emotional regulation, creative association — dominant in second half. Cutting morning sleep disproportionately sacrifices REM.

THE 12 COMMANDMENTS OF SLEEP

1. Fixed wake time — even weekends. 2. Dark room (blackout). 3. Cool room 18–20°C. 4. No screens 90 min pre-bed. 5. Caffeine cutoff 10h before bed. 6. No alcohol within 3h of bed. 7. No large meals within 2h. 8. Exercise daily — not within 2h of bed. 9. Morning sunlight within 60 min of wake. 10. Wind-down ritual 60 min pre-bed. 11. Bed = sleep only. 12. Stress journal before bed to offload cognitive load.

SUPPLEMENTS: EVIDENCE-BASED

Magnesium Glycinate (300–400mg, 1h before bed): GABA modulation, reduces cortisol. Strong evidence. Glycine (3g): Drops core temp, improves NREM. Good evidence. L-Theanine (200mg): Alpha wave induction. Good evidence. Melatonin (0.5mg): Timing signal only, not sleep inducer — take 1–2h before target sleep. Avoid: Alcohol, Benadryl (suppresses REM), cannabis (blocks REM).
