This network is dormant. It has never carried a paid campaign, billed an advertiser, or paid a creator. Delivery stays off until platform_settings.ad_network_enabled is enabled, and no member is owed anything until platform_settings.creator_earnings_enabled is enabled and terms stating the rate and payment schedule are published.
