### city stretch, emblem third column (the 868/539 flip), backdrop 2x cap (2026-11-25)

**1. `city.html` stretch (about 1.45x).** `drawCity` measured `offsetWidth` while the approval guard hid the page, fell back to 600 px, and drew a 1200-wide store into an 867 px box. A `ResizeObserver` on the canvas now refits the one running loop (it re-calls `OmegaCanvas.fit`, it does not start another loop); building positions are fractions of the width so a refit keeps them in the box. Measured (dpr 2): control on `main`, store 1200 wide for a 1262 / 867 px box (stretch 0.475 / 0.692 on x); fixed, 2524 / 1734 wide, stretch 1.000 at both.

**2. The intermittent 868 vs 539 px content column was a race, not a flip.** Repeated loads (1280 px wide, 40 each): contacts 1 load in 40 and fasting 3 in 40 came out 1184 px, the rest 820 / 855 px; physiology stayed 855. In the normal case `.shell` held a third child: the `<div data-page-emblem>` written after `</main>`, a 329-364 px flex column (CLAUDE.md section 4). `omega-page-emblem.js reseat()` moves it into main, but only `if(!p.querySelector('aside, .side, #omega-side')) return;`, and `nav.js` injects that sidebar later, so the result depended on which finished first. Twelve pages lost 329-364 px of content width almost every load (clarity, contacts, fasting, graph-admin, intelligence, lab, mentors, mindmap, passport, physiology, principles, vocabulary). Five more had the same source layout and were winning the race (achievements, charter, cosmos, notes, projects). All 17 placeholders now sit just before `</main>`. After: 90 of 90 loads at 1184 px on physiology, contacts and fasting. `scripts/tests/test_emblem_stays_in_main.py` pins it, with a planted-violation case.
