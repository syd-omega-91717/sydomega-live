KNOWLEDGE GRAPH ARCHITECTURE
ENTITY GRAPH
The Knowledge Graph is a Wikipedia-inspired entity network mapping the sovereign platform's concepts. Nodes represent tracks, elements, gates, and concepts. Edges represent directed relationships (governs, enables, leads to, powers).
NODE TYPES
Four node types: TRACK (12 sovereign knowledge tracks), ELEMENT (9 platform elements), GATE (12 authority thresholds), and CONCEPT (formulas, lattice definitions, and structural constants).
CANVAS RENDERING
The graph is rendered in real-time using the HTML5 Canvas API. Nodes are positioned in rows by type. Edge arrows indicate relationship direction. Click any node to see its connections in the detail panel.
KNOWLEDGE AXIS A
Engaging with the Knowledge Graph contributes to your Axis A (Knowledge) score. Academy modules, lore study, and research submissions are tracked as knowledge events that advance your sovereign progression.
