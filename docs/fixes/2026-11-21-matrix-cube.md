### The map gets the real 3-D matrix cube, with figures from the canon (checklist item e) (2026-11-21)

**Why.** Plan §9 item (e): "a 3-D matrix/cube element". Board-02's centre is a lit 9×9×9 lattice. `omega-sculpture.js` already has the `matrix` scene (one `InstancedMesh`, 729 nodes, a Crystal-Omega at the centre). The risk was the boards' "729 nodes" and similar figures: the plan forbids drawing numbers the platform cannot back (CLAUDE.md 8.1 class 9). The scene's node colours are decorative (cool edge shell, a random 6% bright), so the panel must not imply progress.

**Fix.** `omega-one-shell.js` adds a "The matrix" panel under the relations: a `data-omega-sculpture="matrix"` mount (the module owns WebGL), then facts printed from `window.OmegaCanon.structure` (loaded from `omega-canon.json` by `bg.js`): "9 × 9 × 9 = 729 nodes" per phase, "12 × 12 = 104,976 nodes", the three axes. The shell contains none of those figures, and it prints nothing unless the canon agrees with itself (`depth^axes == nodes_per_track_per_phase`, `tracks × phases × per == total`); otherwise "no figures are shown". The panel says "The lit nodes are decorative. This shows the structure, not your progress." The cube is inert (`pointer-events:none`; its own click would leave the one-page shell), with a real link to `#/p/matrix` (or `/matrix.html`). It is hidden on phones (`display:none !important`; the module writes an inline `display:block`), and hidden if the module never draws it.

**Verified.** Real Chromium at 1440×900: a 278 px lattice with the Crystal-Omega and a cyan shell, facts "Each phase 9 × 9 × 9 = 729 nodes / Tracks × phases 12 × 12 = 104,976 nodes / Axes Knowledge (A) · Mastery (B) · Contribution (C)". `scripts/tests/test_omega_matrix_panel.py` (7 tests): the canon is self-consistent; the drawn cube (`var S = 9`, three `for` axes in the scene) equals the canon's depth and axes; the shell contains none of the figures. Mutation-checked: a typed-in "729" in the shell is caught, and changing the scene to `S = 8` is caught. `scripts/contract-suite.py` 86 gates, 0 failing; `scripts/tests` 1927 OK; `type-scale`, `omega-registry --check`, `audit-webgl-ownership` pass. Browser checks added to `scripts/shell-check.js` (live and painting gold + cyan, figures equal the canon fetched at run time, decorative note, idle when the map is hidden, no WebGL, module blocked, phone); result recorded below.

**Cost, measured.** Same headless Chromium (software rasteriser), page fps over 4 s, two runs each: `main` 8.3 / 7.8 fps with the module reporting 47–49 ms per frame; with the cube 6.3 / 6.5 fps and 93–95 ms. So the cube roughly doubles the module's frame cost and costs about 20% of page frame rate *in this environment*, which is slow in both cases (the module's own governor reports `degraded:true` on `main`). `data-sculpt-bloom="off"` on the cube moved it to 88–92 ms: inside the noise, kept because it is free, not claimed as a fix. A real GPU is not measured.

The lattice is not wired to member data: that would need a real per-node data model, which does not exist. Edge shell/gold interior/6% bright nodes use `Math.random()` (unseeded, in the module), so two renders differ slightly.
