OBSERVATORY ARCHITECTURE
GOOGLE SRE MODEL
The Observatory implements Google's Site Reliability Engineering model — SLO (Service Level Objectives), error budgets, and postmortem incident logs. Error budget consumed drives release decisions: GREEN (<20%) releases go; RED (≥50%) halts all releases.
CORE WEB VITALS
LCP (Largest Contentful Paint ≤ 2,500ms), INP (Interaction to Next Paint ≤ 200ms), and CLS (Cumulative Layout Shift ≤ 0.10) are the three Google ranking signals. Pulled from the platform_metrics table, 75th percentile field data.
SLO SURFACES
Five service surfaces tracked: Auth Service (99.9%), Matrix Engine (99.9%), Vault Service (99.5%), Chatbot/AI (99.0%), and Payment (99.99% — dormant until Phase II). Each surface has its own 4-week error budget window.
INCIDENT POSTMORTEM
All incidents are logged to the incidents table with severity (P0–P4), title, status (open/resolved), and TTR (time to resolve in minutes). Incidents are filed immediately, postmortems are written within 48 hours of resolution. The goal is zero open incidents.
