### The character is alive: a 3-D scene driven by the member's real gates, element and mana (2026-11-15)

**Why.** The owner wants the platform's characters alive, animated, "more than 3D". The 3-D layer (`omega-sculpture.js`, vendored three.js, one WebGL context blitted into each mount) already existed, with a `signet`, `ascension`, `matrix` and others, but nothing drew *the member's own character*. Its data-binding attributes could not do it: `data-sculpt-pulse`, `-tier-progress`, `-tier-color` and `-mastery-scores` are read once at mount into `m` but the scene is built with `{accent, state, el}` only, so the `opt.tierProgress` / `opt.masteryScores` branches inside `ascension` and `elements` are never reached; and the other binding path, `omega-sculpture-dataviz.js`, reads `profile.current_tier`, `current_tier_progress` and `rank`, none of which is a `profiles` column (`live-schema.json`; no migration defines them), so tier progress there is always 0. Recorded in `GAP_ANALYSIS.md` §S, not changed here.

**What.** New scene `SCENES.character` in `omega-sculpture.js`, which reads its attributes **every frame** (data arrives after mount): a core in the sign's element colour, a ring of twelve gate nodes (gate 1 at the top, clockwise) lit for each gate really passed, and five satellites, one per mana component, sized by its value. A component with no recorded source (CREATIVE) is a hollow wireframe ghost, never a small value; unknown gates draw no lit node; the core never goes dark (floor) and its glow follows the mean of the sourced components. Colour is resolved from the canon palette (`OmegaRealm.palette` via `elementColour`), with no copy of the nine colours or the signs in the scene. `omega-character-card.js` gains `bootSigil`, which sets the mount's attributes from the same rules as the card (gates from authority by the platform formula; mana from the new `OmegaRelearnSigilMana.fetchMana`) and writes one plain line under the picture saying what it shows. `fetchMana` is now the single owner of the member-activity read (4 own-rows queries over 30 days); `omega-life.js` calls it instead of carrying its own copy, so `life.html` and `character.html` cannot disagree. Mounted at the top of the IDENTITY tab of `character.html`; the page now loads `omega-relearn-sigil-mana.js`.

**Verified.** Headless Chromium (software GL), `character.html`: with a stub member (Aries, axes 3/3/3) the scene is `live: true`, `fallback: false`, attributes `gates=2`, `element=Fire`, `mana=0.2,0.4,-,0.07,0.1` and the note reads "2 of 12 gates lit · mana 19/100 ... FOCUS 20 · LEARNING 40 · CREATIVE — · COLLABORATION 7 · EXECUTION 10" (identical to the `life.html` panel for the same stub); with an empty stub `mana=0,0,-,0,0` and the note says 0/100; 0 page errors; screenshots read: orange fire core, top two gate nodes lit, the rest dim, four solid satellites and one hollow ghost. A first render put the first gates at the bottom (angle origin); caught in the screenshot and fixed. Phone 390x844: no horizontal overflow, scene live. `test_character_card.py` 14 tests (scene reads attributes inside `update`, ghost for null, no sign/element literals in the scene, wiring), `test_omega_life_surface.js` and `test_omega_relearn_sigil_mana.js` updated for the single owner and pass.

**Not done / not verified.** Software WebGL only: the harness reports `degraded: true` (24 ms frames); real GPUs, phones and the reduced-motion path were not looked at (the scene stills the motion under `prefers-reduced-motion`, untested here). Stubbed Supabase only: real members' gates and mana were not seen. The picture shows state, not rewards: no sound, no interaction, no per-sign model; those are not built. The existing `ascension` and `elements` binding paths and `omega-sculpture-dataviz.js` remain dead (see GAP).
