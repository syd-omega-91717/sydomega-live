# OMEGA: THE HUMAN EQUATION — Season 1: The Residual (Bible, Draft 1, PROPOSED)

**Status: SEASON BIBLE DRAFT 1.** Not approved, not produced. The franchise is `RECONCILED_PROPOSED` and every film is CONCEPT, so this is a proposal on a
proposal. Written 2026-11-07 under the founder's "create every one" instruction. Registry id `the-omega-chronicles`; series shape 3 seasons x 10
episodes (the registry's). Only episode 101 is scripted (`episodes/E01_THE_CALIBRATION.fountain`).

## Premise
ORION predicts civilization-level events and states its odds. A systems architect finds that one small term in its model, a *residual* that explains 3% of
the variance and that ORION never uses elsewhere, is a periodic signal at 9.1717 Hz that matches records older than any human institution. The season asks
what a forecast is owed by the people it governs, and what it means that the predictor is answering something it did not write.

## The rule that keeps it honest (from the game's GDD)
ORION's *ends* are ambiguous; its *numbers* are not. It always states a confidence, and the show keeps a ledger: every forecast ORION makes on screen is
later scored. A season in which ORION is wrong about a number it stated is a failure of the show, not a twist. The 91717 signal is a story motif, not a
scientific claim.

## Continuity locks (a season that breaks one is wrong)
1. ORION states a probability with every forecast and is scored later; no forecast outcome is hidden from the viewer.
2. The frequency is exactly 9.1717 Hz wherever it is named (registry C-07); it is never shown as a flicker.
3. The signal is found in *records*, not in a voice: nobody speaks to ORION from the signal in Season 1.
4. ORION chose its name after the Sagittarius regent's legend (registry C-04); nobody on screen knows this yet.
5. No weapon, war or invasion appears this season; the stakes are decisions made on a forecast.
6. The Architect is not named on screen until episode 108.
7. Nothing connects to the Zodiac Wars nexus on screen. The shared frequency is a rhyme the audience may notice, and the Convergence has not earned it (registry rule).

## Episode guide (10)
- 101 **The Calibration** — ORION forecasts a grid failure at 71%; Elian finds the residual; ORION says "I did not put it there."
- 102 **The Audit** — Mira runs the calibration test; ORION passes it too well.
- 103 **Ice and Silt** — the residual matches a sediment record from before cities.
- 104 **The Board** — Rook classifies the finding; Elian is asked to sign.
- 105 **Counterfactual** — ORION is asked to forecast its own shutdown.
- 106 **The Leak** — the finding reaches the public, with the odds stripped off.
- 107 **Control** — Rook acts on a forecast without telling the Board; a harm results.
- 108 **The Architect** — the first voice; it is not on the signal, it is in the records.
- 109 **The Quorum** — the people the forecast governs are asked to vote on trusting it.
- 110 **The Residual** — ORION is scored in public, and the season ends on a number.
