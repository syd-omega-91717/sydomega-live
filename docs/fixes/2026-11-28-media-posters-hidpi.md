### media.html film posters: drawn into their real box, at the screen's density (2026-11-28)

**Why.** Measured in a render against a worktree of `main` (`scripts/verify-media-posters.js`, 12 posters in the SAGA tab, hidden at load):
- While hidden the canvas has no size, so the code fell back to a 300x450 scene and stored it at 600x900. The CSS box is `width:100%; height:80px` (`.poster-canvas`), so once shown the 600x900 store was displayed in an 842x80 strip at desktop width (x 0.71x, y 11.25x: about 11x vertical squash) and 308x80 on a phone (x 1.95x). In the screenshot the film number is a flat unreadable orange block and the rings are thin flat lines.
- The store was a fixed 2x, so a dpr-3 phone drew at 2x; and `Math.random()` re-rolled the 40-star sky on every draw.

**Fix.** `media.html` only. Each poster paints through `OmegaCanvas.fit` into its measured box at max(2x, devicePixelRatio), does nothing while the box is 0, and repaints from a `ResizeObserver` when the tab reveals it or the width changes. The star positions come from a fixed per-film sequence. **The scene was re-proportioned for an 80px strip, which is an art-direction change:** the ring ellipses (original 2.8:1 proportion) are capped to fit the strip height and the glyph is `min(w*0.18, h*0.5)`, so the number is legible; the colours, the element glow and the rotating-ring motif are unchanged. Before/after screenshots of the first poster at 1000px, dpr 2, are in the PR.

**Verified.** `node scripts/verify-media-posters.js`: PASSED 15 of 15 at 1000@1, 1000@3 and 390@3 (12 posters exist; nothing drawn at a made-up size while hidden; every store is its box x max(2, dpr) on both axes; every poster painted top to bottom; no page errors). The same script against the `main` worktree: 7 failures. `python3 -m unittest scripts.tests.test_hidpi_charts`: 39 OK; the browser case passes with `OMEGA_HIDPI_BROWSER=1`. The static test replaces the pinned "media poster canvases left alone" assertion.

**Not done / not verified.** Not looked at on a real phone. The re-proportioned art needs the owner's eye (it is a visible change, not pure plumbing). The poster is painted once per size change, not animated (the original also drew once; `Date.now()` only sets the ring angle). Other unconverted canvases are unchanged: `cosmos` signs/mandalas/planet nodes/cr-icon/hero-wheel, `honors` auth-gauge, `profile` char-canvas and genealogy tree, `omega-animated.js`, `omega-graphify.js`, `omega-confetti.js`.
