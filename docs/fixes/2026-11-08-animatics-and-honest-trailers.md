### Animatics, and the trailers page stops claiming videos that do not exist (2026-11-08)

**Why.** The user asked for movies, episodes and series "at high visual effects, not only scripts and texts", for repeated tags to go, and for a view of which pages should be one. Measuring first: `trailers.html` had 11 cards, 9 invented running times (2:14, 1:58 ...), 6 identical "OFFICIAL TRAILER" tags, a year (2024) and 11 "WATCH" buttons that have **no handler**; its DEMOS panel claimed a 12-minute walkthrough and its ANIMATICS panel a 0:45 "Twelve Gates" animatic. No video or animation was ever attached. That is fabricated data presented as fact (CLAUDE.md 8.1 class 9) and the source of the repeated tag.

**Fix.** Those three panels are one honest panel that says so and points to the real thing. The real thing is `omega-animatic.js`: a procedural animatic of the cold open of each of six series' first episode, a distinct generative scene per franchise (nine rings and the white door; seven stones; a forecast fan with the residual wave; dig strata and an inscription read one sign at a time; a star map with fleets and a rising Drift bar; a lit wall and a ledger), letterboxed 2.39:1 with a vignette, the **actual spoken lines** of each script as kinetic captions (Soul's "One bell. One tongue. One master we did not save.", Orion and Elian's exchange, Nadia and Samir at the dig), a title card, and a synthesized score that is off until the viewer turns it on (the 9.1717 Hz sub tone only for Zodiac Wars and Omega, the two franchises canon gives it). It lives as a **SCREENING ROOM tab in the owner-only Studio Library**, not as a new page, so it reuses the library's gate and data and adds nothing to the menu. It is labelled in every frame: ANIMATIC, PROCEDURAL, NOT FOOTAGE, NO VOICE ACTING.

**Judged by looking.** The first background wash was a muddy brown over every frame (a gradient that interpolated through a half-transparent colour); found by looking at the frames, not by a number, and fixed with a dark base, a soft centre glow and a vignette. The "picture changes over time" metric was first brightness-based and failed on dark frames; it now counts the percentage of pixels that changed.

**Verified.** `test_animatic.py` (8 tests). In a real browser at 1280 and 375 wide every franchise was rendered at five moments: frames are drawn, the picture changes (2 to 95 percent of pixels between moments), captions show the script's lines and then the title, PLAY/PAUSE/RESTART and the scrubber work, sound is off by default, no overflow, no page errors. The Human Frontier's cold open has no spoken line, so it shows its premise and says so.

**Not done / not verified.** This is not a film: no actor, voice, music or shot exists. The score was never listened to (this session has no audio device), so it is untested by ear. Visual quality was judged from screenshots by one reviewer; the owner's eye is the real test. The six series' episodes are the short drafts. Two parallel film catalogues remain on the platform (see `docs/PAGE_CONSOLIDATION.md`).
