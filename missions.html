◕ STREAK SCIENCE
Habit research (Clear, 2018; Duhigg, 2012) shows a minimum of 66 days of consistent behavior to form automatic sovereignty. The streak system enforces a daily commitment signal. Missing one day resets the streak counter — no grace period. Consistency compounds: a 30-day streak is worth 500 XP bonus via the Sovereign Trial legendary quest.
◈ ELEMENT ALIGNMENT
Each mission is aligned to a sovereign element — FIRE (action), WATER (stillness), WIND (knowledge), METAL (discipline), MIND (study), SOUL (expression), SPACE (exploration), COSMOS (connection). Completing missions across all 8 elements drives Element Convergence — a legendary quest requiring balanced development above the 80th percentile in all sovereign DNA attributes simultaneously.
