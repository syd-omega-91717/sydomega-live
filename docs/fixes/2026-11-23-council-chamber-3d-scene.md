### The 12-executor council chamber as a 3-D scene (SCENES.council) (2026-11-23)

**Why.** The owner directed (2026-11-23) that the twelve-executor council chamber be emblem-based, rotating and three-dimensional ("3D and 5D"), and be a door into the platform's multi-universe. The roster page's COUNCIL tab drew the twelve executors as plain octahedra on a ring (`SCENES.agents`), with no emblem, name or per-executor destination (all twelve nodes went to `/agents.html`).

**Fix.** `SCENES.council` in `omega-sculpture.js` (mount: `data-omega-sculpture="council"`): a round chamber with the Omega signet on a dais and twelve seats. Each seat is a pedestal carrying a coin that turns about its vertical axis and shows that executor's own zodiac emblem on both faces, tinted by the executor's element, inside two tilted armillary rings that turn about other axes, with a name plate. The camera orbits slowly and the cursor steers it. The roster, signs, elements and destinations come from `omega-agents.json` (the file `agents.html` and `omega-constellation.js` already use); element colours come from `OmegaRealm.palette`; inline lists are only the fallback. Every coin is a door to that executor's own page (privacy, vault, search, family, command, compliance, contracts, prediction, vision, intelligence, academy, family#heritage), and the host also emits a real `<a>` per coin for keyboard and screen-reader users. Mounted as the COUNCIL tab's stage on `agents.html` (replacing the plain ring there; `SCENES.agents` stays and is still used on `sculpture.html`) and as its own stage on `sculpture.html`. Reduced motion draws one still frame; a narrow mount pulls the camera back so the whole chamber stays in frame; with WebGL absent a flat ring of twelve discs around the Omega is drawn.

**What "3D and 5D" means in this build (owner to confirm).** "5D" is not a rendering mode, so it is defined here rather than implied: three dimensions of space (x, y, z), one of time (coins, rings and camera turn), and one of state (the same live task-activity signal the `agents` and `signet` scenes already read, `OmegaSculptureDataViz.getActiveTaskCount`, quickens the turning and lights the coins). Nothing else is claimed. Recorded in `OMEGA_TAXONOMY.md` section 9 so a different meaning can replace it.

**Bugs found while building, by looking and by testing.** (1) The first render had the camera too close (floor spilling out of the frame, name plates hidden behind nearer coins) and a teal floor and dais from the scene's cyan fill light; fixed with a farther camera, matte materials and smaller plates. (2) The cyan fill washed every coin to the same mint colour; the faces are now self-lit by their own emblem so each element keeps its tint. (3) The host builds its real `<a>` list once, from the links present at wiring time, but the roster arrives after a fetch, so the anchors were stuck on the placeholder `/agents.html`. The scene now exposes `ready`, keeps `links` empty until the roster resolves (or a 4 s wait gives up), then opens the doors. (4) The glyphs were committed as literal characters with an invisible variation selector; converted to `\u` escapes.

**Verified.** `node scripts/verify-council-scene.js` 14/14 (SwiftShader WebGL, canvas pixels and DOM): coin-face area and colour families on the showcase page, the roster tab and a 375 px mount; the picture changes in 1.5 s; one still frame under reduced motion; twelve `<a>` doors equal to `omega-agents.json` in name and destination and in order; clicking a coin navigates to one of those pages; the no-WebGL fallback draws; 0 page errors. **Mutation-tested, and the suite was tightened because the first version was not good enough:** with six seats hidden, or every executor one tint, the first version still passed 14/14 (its sector/hue checks were satisfied by the floor rim and the fill light). The measures were replaced by two that those regressions actually change (bright coin-face area; colour families holding at least 0.15 % of the canvas), taking the best of eight frames because a coin face is bright only when it faces the camera. Calibrated: good desktop 5.4-5.7 % and 6 families (threshold 3.5 % and 4); six seats hidden at most 2.3 %; one tint at most 3 families. Final run against the three mutations: seats hidden fails the area checks on the showcase and the tab; doors never opened fails the doors and click-through checks; one tint fails the colour-family check. `scripts/tests/test_council_scene.py` 10 static tests. An earlier blob-counting approach was abandoned (the glyph splits a coin, neighbouring coins merge).

**Not done / not verified.** (1) Software-rendered only: frame rate on real devices is unmeasured (about 100 meshes and 24 canvas textures). (2) "Each task takes the member to another dimension" is NOT built here: this change is the chamber only. `omega:task-complete` is dispatched from `habits.html` alone, and `bg.js`'s fetch recorder does not report which request settled, so a platform-wide per-task effect needs a small addition there; it is the next piece. (3) The whole platform is not converted to rotating 3-D emblems; this adds the council chamber, which is where the twelve executors live. (4) `council.html` is the "Decision Engine" page, a different thing; it was not changed. (5) Not deployed.
