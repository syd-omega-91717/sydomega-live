SOVEREIGN SCIENCE OF MENTAL MASTERY
SPEED ARITHMETIC
Mental arithmetic activates prefrontal cortex pathways governing executive decision-making. Regular practice compresses reaction time across all cognitive domains — the forge effect. Score = (correct × 100) + (60 − elapsed) × 5.
SEQUENCE RECOGNITION
Pattern recognition is the foundation of sovereign intelligence. Sequences tested: geometric progression (×2), perfect squares (n²), triangular numbers, Fibonacci series, cubic sequences (n³). Speed bonus = max(50, 200 − elapsed×2).
MEMORY MATRIX
Working memory capacity directly correlates with fluid intelligence. The 12-symbol grid forces chunking and encoding under time pressure. Perfect recall awards a 100-point bonus on top of the per-symbol score (12 × 20 = 240 base).
CIPHER BREAK
Caesar cipher: each letter is shifted by a fixed offset (2–6). Frequency analysis reveals the shift — E is the most common letter in English. Score = similarity × 300 + (similarity > 90% ? 100 : 0). Maximum possible: 400 points.
CREATIVE FIRE
Divergent thinking is measured by fluency (idea count) and originality. 3-minute ideation sessions under open prompts maximize creative output. Score = ideas × 20 + remaining_seconds × 2, capped at 300.
