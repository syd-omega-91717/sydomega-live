### Last Galactic Empire, Slice 0, and the registry's game statuses (2026-11-08)

**Why.** The user asked for the next playable game and for thought about the whole project. Reading the registry showed the whole-project problem: `config/omega-studio-registry.json` still listed *Omega: Civilization 91717* as DESIGNED although its page was built, tested and merged (#1208), and `test_studio_canon.py` and `test_studio_library.py` asserted that only Myth of the Stones' Origins could be a PROTOTYPE, so the tests were holding the registry out of date. Last Galactic Empire's GDD (section 5) had concrete acceptance criteria and no build.

**Fix.** `omega-galactic-logic.js` (pure rules), `omega-galactic-game.js` and `game-last-galactic-empire.html` (screen), `scripts/tests/test_galactic_logic.py` (16 tests). Both games are now PROTOTYPE with a `build` file in the registry; the two canon tests now require every PROTOTYPE to have an existing build file and GDD instead of naming one franchise.

**What measuring changed.** My first version let a scripted Court win 100% of 200 seeds in eight seasons (peace needed trust of only 40, one freedom law reaches that in three seasons) and the enemy's fixed doctrine made guarding unbeatable. Fixes: peace needs trust 60, starting distrust is higher, the Court starts poorer; the AI now commits its orders from the board as it stood at the start of the season and picks the doctrine that beats what the defenders are on now, so the Court must out-think it by switching first. Final measurement over 200 seeds: a Court that manages doctrines wins 100%, one that never touches doctrines wins about 68% (the rest lose the capital), an idle Court loses the capital every time (about 3 seasons).

**Bug found by driving the real page.** The 7-column fleets table was wider than a phone, so the order selects were cut off ("ho"); fleets are now stacked cards with visible labels.

**Verified.** 16 rules tests; `./scripts/ci-local.sh` all 35 blocking checks. In headless Chromium at 1280 and 375 wide the scripted Court played all 13 seasons through the law buttons, doctrine and order selects, reinforce checkboxes and grievance buttons; the final owners, fleets, Drift, trust, treasury and history equal the offline run exactly; an invalid plan disables END SEASON with the reason; clicking a neighbouring system orders the selected fleet; reload restores; a tampered save is discarded; no overflow, duplicate ids or page errors.

**Not done / not verified.** No human has played it. The enemy is deterministic, so a scripted Court that finds the doctrine counter wins every seed; randomness is limited to grievances. Slice 0 is the Court only (GDD open question 2), runs on the main thread (a whole campaign takes about 0.5 ms here) and saves to localStorage; no Web Worker, no audio, no multiplayer (UNAVAILABLE). The capital is shown as "The imperial capital" (canon leaves it open); the other 19 system names are invented proposals.
