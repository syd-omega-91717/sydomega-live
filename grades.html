GRADE ARCHITECTURE
12 SOVEREIGN GRADES
The 12 grades map directly to the 12 gates of the sovereign lattice. Each grade is earned, not assigned — it requires reaching the corresponding authority threshold and satisfying the listed prerequisite conditions.
AUTHORITY FORMULA
AUTH = √(A³+B³+C³) × φ/e. At A=B=C=9, AUTH = 27.8367 (APEX). At A=B=C=0, AUTH = 0. The formula rewards balanced growth across all three axes — stacking one axis alone yields less than distributing effort evenly.
GRADE PERMANENCE
Grades earned are permanently recorded in the Sovereign Codex. Authority can fluctuate, but once a grade threshold is crossed and all prerequisites met, the grade is sealed and cannot be revoked except by the platform owner under extraordinary circumstances.
GRADE XII — APEX
Apex Sovereign (Grade XII) requires A=B=C=9, AUTH=27.8367, all certificates, and lattice completion. It is the only state in the 9×9×9 Matrix where every node has been reached. Fewer than 1% of members are expected to achieve it in any given cycle.
