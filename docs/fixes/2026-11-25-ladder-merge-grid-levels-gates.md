### grid, levels and gates merged into one ladder page (2026-11-25)

**Why.** `grid`, `levels` and `gates` were three views of one object: the 12 gates of `OmegaCanon.authority`. I had parked this merge as risky ("levels carries canon numbers", "gates has a 3-D corridor and a log"); the owner asked for it to be built anyway. Reading the pages showed the risk was a divergent-copies problem (CLAUDE.md 8.1 class 8), not a conflict: `levels` read the canon's table; `grid` carried its own thresholds and the 12 signs, abbreviated ("SAGITT.", "CAPRIC."); `gates` carried thresholds to two decimals (23.20 where the canon says 23.1972) and gate 1 at 0.

**Fix.**
- `levels.html` is the survivor, now eight tabs: ALL LEVELS, STRUCTURE (plus the lattice statistics), MY LEVEL (plus the sovereign path and gate progression), GRID, GATES (KPIs, the 3-D corridor, the 12 gate cards), LOG ATTEMPT, HISTORY, SCIENCE (the levels, lattice and gates cards). Page-own and still hard-coded: the 12 gate curriculum themes, descriptions and domain colours, the roman numerals. Gate unlocking keeps its old rule with canon numbers: gate n opens at the canon threshold of the rung below it, gate 1 from the start, gate 12 at APEX itself.
