# GALACTIC THRONES — Season 1: The Unregistered Fleet (Bible, Draft 1, PROPOSED)

**Status: SEASON BIBLE DRAFT 1.** Not approved, not produced. The franchise is `PROPOSED` (new to the canon) and all films are CONCEPT. Written 2026-11-07.
Registry id `the-last-galactic-empire`; series shape 4 seasons x 10 episodes (the registry's). Only episode 101 is scripted
(`episodes/E01_THE_UNREGISTERED_FLEET.fountain`).

## Premise
Interstellar civilizations formed the Empire to end endless war. Generations later the Empire holds the one thing its Founding Compact forbade: a fleet that
its neighbours cannot see. Season 1 is a political thriller about an audit. A strategist finds one ship that is not in the register, and every institution
around him has a reason not to have noticed.

## Tone and method
Space opera played as procedure: ledgers, seating charts, oaths, a coronation that is a budget. Battles are rare and expensive; the show's violence is mostly
a signature on a form. Each episode names one clause of the Founding Compact and breaks it.

## Anchor to the wider canon
The registry anchors this franchise at saga chapter 11, where the 9.1717 Hz frequency is detected in seven other civilisations (conflict C-14). Season 1
does **not** mention the frequency; it only builds the empire that will detect it. No character here knows of the Zodiac Houses or the Stones.

## Continuity locks
1. The Founding Compact has exactly ten clauses; Season 1 breaks one per episode (101 is clause four: "No world shall hold a fleet its neighbours cannot see").
2. The unregistered fleet is real from the first scene; the audience is never asked whether it exists, only who knew.
3. Aethon IV has not been told about it by anyone in the Admiralty. Whether he knew is the season's slow question.
4. No battle before episode 106.
5. Surname Ardent is also used in *The Lost Civilizations* (Nadia Ardent). Recorded as conflict C-26: no relation unless the Convergence earns it.

## Episode guide (10)
- 101 **The Unregistered Fleet** — a jubilee; a register; one ship that is not in it.
- 102 **The Petition** — Seraya's reform petition reaches the Throne Hall; it is read and not answered.
- 103 **Clause Five** — the Empire's courts and who may be tried.
- 104 **The Independent Worlds** — Juno Ardent sets a price for her silence.
- 105 **The Table** — the first meeting of the Empire and the independents; no one signs.
- 106 **Firstlight** — the first shots; fired by the ship nobody registered.
- 107 **The Admiral's Reasons** — Threx explains, and is right about half.
- 108 **The Emperor's Count** — Aethon IV finally counts the fleet in public.
- 109 **The Vote of the Worlds** — the Compact is read aloud before the people it binds.
- 110 **The Seventh Clause** — the season ends on a clause nobody has broken yet.
