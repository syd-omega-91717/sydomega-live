## Premise

Seven primordial stones hold fragments of an ancient power. Civilizations have fought over them for thousands of years without understanding their purpose.

## Where it sits in the chronology

- **The First Convergence** (age-1): Origins of the primordial forces, the seven stones and the earliest celestial territories
- **The Age of Guardians** (age-2): The first guardians emerge; the zodiac houses establish their order
- **The Lost Civilizations** (age-3): Ancient kingdoms find fragments of the original knowledge; several vanish trying to control it

## Recorded conflicts touching this franchise

- **C-01** (name, PROPOSED_RESOLUTION): The brief's 'Kael Draven' collides with Kael Marren (merged draft). The Myth of the Stones character is renamed Torin Draven; the Rise of Aries journeyman Draven became Brannock.
- **C-09** (lore, PROPOSED_RESOLUTION): The seven stones are a mortal taxonomy: the guardians sorted the nine elements into seven aspects (Creation, Memory, Time, Energy, Matter, Consciousness, Balance). The mismatch is intentional and part of the mystery.
- **C-16** (name, PROPOSED_RESOLUTION): Shared surname Vale is by design: mother and son.
- **C-17** (lore, PROPOSED_RESOLUTION): The Seven Stones shows a nine-ring wall with seven rings inscribed and two blank, seeding the nine-element canon (C-09) without naming the missing two.
- **C-18** (motif, PROPOSED_RESOLUTION): The phrase 'hold the other end' appears in The Seven Stones (Ithren) and The Rise of Aries (Solenne). Treated as a deliberate cross-franchise echo and a Convergence motif.
- **C-19** (chronology, PROPOSED_RESOLUTION): Season 1 of The Chronicles of the Guardians runs in the eleven months before The Seven Stones and ends on the film's first present-day scene, so it keeps the brief's episode titles without retelling the film. Season 2 follows the film.
- **C-20** (scope, PROPOSED_RESOLUTION): The brief's third-person open-world RPG is a design target; the first buildable artefact is a 2D top-down Vertical Slice 0, labelled PROTOTYPE until it meets the release criteria in the GDD. Multiplayer is UNAVAILABLE.
- **C-21** (lore, PROPOSED_RESOLUTION): The Season 1 episode 1 beat sheet says two niches are filled in Malachar's sanctum, but Continuity Lock 1 says he holds only the Time stone until episode 6 (Energy arrives then). The episode 1 script shows one filled niche (Time) and six empty.
