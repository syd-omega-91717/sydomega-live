HERITAGE SCIENCE
01
EPIGENETICS & HERITAGE
Epigenetic inheritance: experiences of ancestors can alter gene expression in descendants. Holocaust survivors' grandchildren show altered cortisol profiles. Your heritage is not only cultural — it is biological.
02
NARRATIVE IDENTITY
McAdams' life-story theory: identity is a narrative we construct from our past. Knowing your heritage provides narrative raw material — ancestors who overcame adversity become an internal resource for resilience.
03
THE "DO YOU KNOW" SCALE
Marshall Duke and Robyn Fivush found that children who know their family history score higher on 20+ resilience measures. Heritage knowledge is not nostalgia — it is a measurable psychological protective factor.
04
CULTURAL CAPITAL
Bourdieu's cultural capital: inherited tastes, values, and social skills from family. Heritage shapes epistemic assumptions, aesthetic preferences, and social competence in ways that persist across lifetimes.
05
ANCESTRAL WISDOM
Indigenous knowledge systems — encoded in oral tradition, ceremony, and practice — represent thousands of years of environmental adaptation. Sovereign heritage work recovers these ancestral algorithms before they are lost.
06
INTERGENERATIONAL WEALTH
The longest-studied case of intergenerational wealth persistence is 700+ years in Florence (Barone & Mocetti, 2016). Heritage shapes not just culture but economic trajectory — surnames still predict wealth in records from 1427.
