SOVEREIGN NUTRITION PRINCIPLES
PROTEIN SOVEREIGNTY
Target: 0.8–1.2g per pound of bodyweight daily.

Protein is the building block of muscle and the most satiating macronutrient. High-protein diets preserve muscle during fat loss and support cognitive function.

Best sources: eggs, chicken, fish, Greek yogurt, legumes, cottage cheese.
HYDRATION PROTOCOL
Minimum: 35ml × bodyweight(kg) daily.

Even 2% dehydration impairs cognitive performance by up to 20%. The sovereign hydrates before hunger — thirst is already a deficit signal.

Add electrolytes (sodium, potassium, magnesium) for peak cellular hydration.
NUTRIENT TIMING
· Morning: High protein, moderate fat, lower carb → sustained energy, no insulin spike
· Pre-training: Complex carbs + protein 60-90 min before
· Post-training: Fast protein + simple carbs within 30 min
· Evening: Lower calorie, casein protein for overnight muscle repair
SOVEREIGN SUPERFOODS
· Blueberries: BDNF, neuroplasticity, anthocyanins
· Salmon: Omega-3 DHA — brain fuel, inflammation control
· Dark Chocolate 85%+: Flavonoids, vasodilation
· Eggs: Complete protein, choline for memory encoding
· Leafy Greens: Magnesium, folate, nitric oxide precursors
· Bone Broth: Collagen, gut healing, electrolytes
