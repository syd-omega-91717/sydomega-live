### Studio Library: owner-only activation of the Studios content (2026-11-07)

**Why.** The owner asked that the movies, series, episodes, games and characters be real and activatable in the platform. Measured first: none of `movies`, `series`, `characters`, `gaming` or `universe` reads anything from the Studios canon, and the canon, screenplays and scripts lived only under `docs/`, which the site does not serve and the public repository exposes. So nothing in the platform could show any of it.

**Fix.** One generator (`scripts/studio-library.py`) derives a single payload from the registry, the productions file and the script and design files: 7 franchises, 26 films, 62 characters and 10 documents (2 film screenplays, 4 episode scripts, 4 game design documents), about 42,000 words, written to `supabase/functions/studio-library/library-data.ts`. A new owner-only function `studio-library` (same `decideOwner` guard as `studio-canon`, no request input) serves it, and `studio-library.html` shows it: films, series, games and characters with their real status, a screenplay and teleplay reader, design documents, and the one openable build (the Origins prototype). The name `library` was already taken by an unrelated page, so the page is `studio-library`. Registered as `PENDING_DEPLOY` in `config.toml`, the auth registry, the snapshot and the census manifest. Self-review found a platform text clamp (`omega-readmore.js`) truncating screenplay paragraphs mid-word; the reader is now marked `data-no-clamp`.

**Verified.** `test_studio_library.py` (payload current, stale payload caught, inventory equals the registry, every document reachable, statuses are canon statuses, exactly one openable prototype, both owner functions keep the same guard); the full `./scripts/ci-local.sh`; headless Chromium at 1280 and 375 wide with a stubbed owner session: 26/7/7/62 cards, franchise filter, a screenplay read (56 scenes, 627 cues), episode 101 and a design document read, no truncation, no duplicate ids, no overflow, no page errors; and with stubbed refusals the page shows only RESTRICTED, APPROVAL REQUIRED or UNAVAILABLE and none of the library content is in the DOM.

**Not done / not verified.** No video, audio or footage exists or was generated: the library shows written works and one prototype, and says so. `studio-library` has not been called live: after the merge deploys it, the owner path and the non-owner `owner_only` path are the first live checks, and its snapshot stays `PENDING_DEPLOY` until then. The library is only as complete as the canon: 24 of 26 films and 146 of about 150 episodes are not written.
