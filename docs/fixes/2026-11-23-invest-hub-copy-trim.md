### The invest hub says it in fewer words and keeps every honesty claim (2026-11-23)

**Why.** Requested by the owner ("less text and explanations"). Measured first: the money pages carry 2,908 words in long text blocks (13 pages), the invest hub the most (15 blocks, 323 words by the first count): a 33-word lede, fourteen card sentences of 10 to 23 words, and a 49-word "truth boundary". The tempting trim is wrong here: many of those sentences are deliberate disclosures that something is disabled, local-only or not a balance (CLAUDE.md 9), and `config`/tests pin at least one verbatim. What makes a trim safe is that every card already has a status chip under it (`DORMANT · TOKENS DISABLED`, `DISABLED · NO VERIFIED SETTLEMENT`, `USER-LOCAL`, ...) which carries the state, so the sentence only needs to carry the claim a chip cannot.

**Fix.** `invest.html`: the lede 33 to 21 words; thirteen card sentences rewritten to the purpose plus the one claim (for example blockchain: "Asset architecture reference. Tokens are disabled; no on-chain balance is claimed." from 18 words of the same meaning plus filler); the ad-network card only loses "contextual advertising" (its three-part "has not carried a paid campaign, billed an advertiser or paid a creator" stays whole); the truth boundary 49 to 33 words, keeping all three promises (nothing is invented, browser-local data stays local, live state keeps its authorization). The status chips, the status strip above the cards and the test-pinned advertising sentence ("payouts remain disabled pending legal and payment readiness") are untouched. Measured: visible words 496 to 344 (-31%); long prose 354 to 188 (-47%).

**Verified.** `scripts/tests/test_invest_hub_copy.py` (9 tests) pins a word budget (lede at most 25, card sentences at most 14, 20 for the two disclosure-heavy ones, total prose at most 230) and every disclosure that a chip does not hold (11 cards), the chips, the status strip and the truth-boundary promises. Mutation-tested: dropping "no on-chain balance is claimed", "Not a financial tracker" or "billing are disabled" each fails the test, and regrowing the lede fails the budget. Contract suite 95/95, commerce contract, i18n contract and the hub/domain tests pass; rendered at 1280 and 375 px with no horizontal overflow.

(2) The hub repeats the same destinations in four navigation strips above its own content (the domain console, the protected-workflows row, the chip row and the breadcrumb row); those come from platform-wide hub modules, not this page's markup, and were left alone. (3) English only. (4) Not deployed.
