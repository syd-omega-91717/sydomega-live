## 1. The pitch (founder's text, verbatim)

In a universe where the twelve Zodiac Gods rule entire planets and command civilizations across the Solar System, an ancient cosmic war reignites when the Solar Nexus awakens. Humanity becomes caught between Light Zodiac rulers, Shadow Zodiac counterparts, forgotten primordial guardians, and an existential force known as the Null, a self-aware collapse of reality itself.

Planned shape (founder's list): 1 feature film, a 10-episode limited series, a 5-season series, and an online Fighting RPG.

## 3. The Twelve Zodiac Gods (PROPOSED)

Each god rules one world. Each has a **Shadow counterpart**: not an enemy from outside, but the same god as they would be if their domain were pursued without its limit. Light and Shadow are two ways of holding the same power.

| House (sign) | Element | Light ruler | World | Shadow counterpart | What the Shadow is |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Aries | Fire | Ares | Mars | The Unbled | War with no cost counted. |
| Taurus | Metal | Aphrodite | Venus | The Hoarded | Desire that owns what it loves. |
| Gemini | Wind | Hermes | Mercury | The Twice-Told | Every message true to every listener, and false. |
| Cancer | Water | Artemis | The Moon | The Unlit | Protection that becomes confinement. |
| Leo | Fire | Apollo | The Sun's corona | The Glare | Truth so bright it burns the thing it shows. |
| Virgo | Sand | Athena | Ceres | The Perfect | A plan with no room for a person. |
| Libra | Wind | Hera | Juno | The Sworn | Law that outlives its reason. |
| Scorpio | Water | Demeter | Pluto | The Fallow | Grief that lets the harvest rot. |
| Sagittarius | Fire | Zeus | Jupiter | The Unanswerable | Authority that no longer hears a question. |
| Capricorn | Metal | Hestia | Saturn | The Cold Hearth | Duty kept after the family is gone. |
| Aquarius | Wind | Hephaestus | Uranus | The Unfinished | Invention that never asks who it is for. |
| Pisces | Water | Poseidon | Neptune | The Drowned | Memory so deep nothing surfaces. |

**The Solar Accord.** Twelve thousand years ago the Twelve divided the worlds and swore a peace, the Accord. The Accord is not friendship: each god presses its House toward its own idea of sovereignty, which is the argument the Knights inherit (`media.html`, "Wrath of the Gods"). The Nexus waking is the Accord's first real test.

**The thirteenth seat.** Earth belongs to no god. It is the one world the Twelve cannot rule without the consent of those who live on it, which is why the war reaches it.

## 4. The Forgotten Guardians and the Null (PROPOSED)

**The four guardians: Soul, Space, Void, The All.** Before the Twelve, the elements without a House held the Solar System together. When the gods took the five sign-elements (Fire, Water, Wind, Metal, Sand) and divided the planets between them, these four were left without a throne and then without a memory. They are not gods: they are conditions: Soul is what is carried across a death, Space is the distance that lets two things be separate, Void is the room that lets anything be made, The All is the whole that the other eight add up to.

**The Solar Nexus** is the point where all nine elements meet. It has been dormant since the Twelve divided the worlds. Its awakening is the inciting event of the pitch.

**The Null** is not the Void. The Void is empty and receptive: it makes room. The Null is a collapse that has become aware: it does not oppose the elements, it unmakes the difference between them. It speaks only in what each listener most wants to stop being responsible for. Its method is to make each god's Shadow feel like relief.

**Why the war is a war.** The Light rulers believe the Null is an invader. The Shadows believe it is the end of an argument they were losing. The guardians know that the Null is what an unheld Solar System decays into, which means the first war, long ago, was not won: it was paused.

## 6. Structure across the formats (PROPOSED)

The twelve gates (`INITIATE` to `APEX`) give a spine that works at every length.

- **Feature film (1).** One night: the Solar Nexus wakes over Earth. Three Houses (Aries, Libra, Pisces) reach the thirteenth seat first. Ends with the Null speaking for the first time and a single Knight refusing it. Sets the rules; resolves nothing.
- **Limited series (10 episodes).** Ten gates, one per episode, from `INITIATE` (a Knight learns they are a Knight) to `ORACLE`. Each episode follows one House's Light and Shadow. The tenth episode ends on the reveal that the first war was only paused.
- **Series (5 seasons).** Two Houses per season plus one guardian per season, in the order the Nexus wakes them: Season 1 Soul, 2 Space, 3 Void, 4 The All, 5 the Null itself. Season 5 is the cost of ending the pause.
- **Closing note.** The gates `PRIME` and `APEX` are held back for the finale: not the defeat of the Null, but a Light and its Shadow choosing to be the same god.

## 7. Game concept: online Fighting RPG (PROPOSED)

- **Roster.** Twelve Light rulers and twelve Shadows (24 fighters), unlocked in House order, plus the Null as an end encounter. Four guardian "assists" (Soul, Space, Void, The All).
- **Core rule.** A fighter's Shadow is a stance, not a second character: pushing a House's strength past its limit swaps to the Shadow kit, which hits harder and loses the ability to defend. This makes the fiction a mechanic.
- **Progression.** Twelve gates, as on the platform. Rank is earned in play, never bought.
- **Out of scope until the founder decides:** monetisation, any member-facing token reward, matchmaking and moderation. Those are the platform's payment and legal gates (`tokens_enabled` is false); none is implied by this document.
