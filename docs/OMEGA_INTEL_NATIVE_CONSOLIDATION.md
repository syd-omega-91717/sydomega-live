Date: 2026-10-08

## Implemented

The canonical INTEL workspace now includes a native, read-only observation panel over the authenticated member's persisted `omega_platform_events` rows.

It reports only canonical event records that the signed-in member can read. Empty and unavailable states are explicit. No event, KPI, outcome, provider result, or intelligence claim is fabricated.

The existing Knowledge Loom hybrid retrieval remains the canonical retrieval path. Specialist intelligence, research, graph, evidence, and knowledge routes remain stable deep links until their underlying state and actions are safely absorbed.

## Boundary

An event proves that an event row was persisted. It does not prove that the business outcome represented by that event actually occurred outside the platform.
