### Twelve ambient modules load after the page is idle (2026-11-05)

**Why.** Measured 2026-11-05 (`docs/PLATFORM_COMPLEXITY_ANALYSIS.md`): 165 script files (1.99 MB raw) load on every page, `bg.js` injects about 156 modules unconditionally, and at a 4x CPU slowdown (390 px, local server, median of 3) pages take 3.5 to 6.8 s to load with 4.6 to 7.7 s of main-thread blocking. A CPU profile of `journal` and `dashboard` shows where it goes: 1.6 to 2.5 s is page script, 106 to 177 style recalculations and 35 to 88 layouts, the rest is the browser parsing and painting (headless paints in software, so not read closely). Twelve modules are ambient: cinematic layers (3 files), `motion-v3`, voice (3), `music`, `living-art`, `orbital-ring`, `lattice-3d`, `depth-tilt`. Nothing a page needs to paint or respond depends on them.

**Fix.** `bg.js` gets `__omegaIdleAppend(el)`: wait for `load`, then insert through `requestIdleCallback` with a 2.5 s cap (`setTimeout` fallback). The 12 modules' injection lines call it instead of `__omegaAppend`; nothing else about them changed, and `__omegaAppend` still de-duplicates at the moment of insertion. This is safe by evidence, not assumption: the same measurements show most injected scripts already run after `DOMContentLoaded` (about 0.8 s against a 3.5 to 6.8 s load), so these modules already tolerate arriving late. Undo is changing a call back to `__omegaAppend`.

**Verified.** 4x CPU slowdown, same six pages before and after: scripts requested before `load` 171-180 to 159-168 (-7%, 12 fewer); load time -7%, -9%, -4%, -7% on `journal`, `academy`, `life`, `vault`; blocking time -8%, -7%, -4%, -2% on the same four; `gaming` (+1% load) and `dashboard` (+4%) are inside run-to-run noise. All 12 modules still load on `journal`, `dashboard`, `gaming` and `life` (12 of 12), at 0.4 to 2.7 s after load (the 2.5 s cap fires on the busier pages). `node scripts/verify-runtime.js --all`: PASS on all 236 pages. `scripts/tests/test_bg_idle_modules.py` pins the batch.

**Not done / not verified.** This is a small win, not the fix: the 12 modules are about 100 KB of 1.5 MB and the page still requests the same files. Only the order changed. A phone, real network and real GPU paint were not measured. The modules' behaviour was checked only as "loads and nothing throws", not feature by feature (for example the voice controls). The larger costs found are untouched: the five modules that re-scan the page on every mutation (`bottom-stack`, `content`, `9d`, `motion`, `alive`; 45 to 138 ms of `querySelectorAll` each at 4x), `three.js` loading on pages that do not draw 3-D, and about 95 stylesheets per page.
