SIGIL DERIVATION
Your sovereign sigil is algorithmically derived from your profile data. No two sigils are identical — the combination of zodiac sign, element, and authority score produces a unique geometric mark for every member.
CANVAS RENDERING
Sigils are drawn in real-time using the HTML5 Canvas API. A six-pointed star forms the primary frame; the element glyph anchors the centre; the authority value rotates around the outer perimeter in 8px monospace text.
AUTHORITY EVOLUTION
As your authority score increases from 0 to 27.8367, the sigil's perimeter inscription updates to reflect your current value. The star geometry and outer rings remain constant; the text evolves with your progress.
DOWNLOAD & EXPORT
The Download PNG function uses Canvas.toDataURL() to export your sigil at full resolution. The exported file is a 220×220 or 300×300 PNG — suitable for use as a profile mark, avatar, or certificate seal.
