REALM ARCHITECTURE
THE SOVEREIGN REALM
The Sovereign Realm is a 3-D interactive element sphere rendered on a WebGL canvas via Three.js (OmegaRealm). Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom. Each of the nine elements occupies a sovereign position on the sphere, glowing with its spectral color. Your zodiac sign determines your native element at initialization.
NINE ELEMENTS
Fire, Water, Wind, Metal, Sand, Soul, Space, Void, and The All — nine sovereign elements that form the triad system. Three per triad: Primal (Fire/Water/Wind), Middle (Metal/Sand/Soul), Supreme (Space/Void/The All). The apex element — The All — integrates all nine at Gate XII.
ELEMENT AFFINITY
Your zodiac sign assigns your native element at onboarding. Aries/Leo/Sagittarius map to Fire. Cancer/Scorpio/Pisces to Water. Gemini/Libra/Aquarius to Wind. Taurus/Capricorn to Metal. Virgo to Sand. The sphere initialises centred on your element, glowing at maximum intensity.
REALM ENGINE
OmegaRealm.mount(canvas, element) initialises the Three.js scene. OmegaRealm.setElement(elem) shifts focus to any of the nine elements. OmegaAmbient plays elemental audio resonance when an element is selected. All rendering is client-side — no server round-trips after initial load.
