GOVERNANCE & COMPLIANCE · THE SCIENCE
SOVEREIGN CONSTITUTION
The Platform Constitution is an immutable legal and operational document. Five Articles define absolute ownership, governance structure, frequency lock, and the non-deletion clause. No consensus can override the Architect's veto.
HASH-CHAIN AUDIT
Every database write is hash-chained: each record's hash includes the previous record's hash, creating a cryptographic audit trail. Tampering with any historical record invalidates all subsequent hashes — mathematically detectable.
MULTI-JURISDICTIONAL
Six jurisdictional frameworks apply simultaneously: GDPR (EU), CCPA (California), MiCA (crypto), UK GDPR, Lebanon Cybercrime Law, and a contractual global default. Each framework has specific data rights, consent requirements, and financial rules.
3-OF-5 MULTI-SIG
Treasury actions above threshold require cryptographic signatures from 3 of 5 designated keyholders. The Architect holds 1 key permanently. This prevents single points of failure, insider threats, and unauthorized fund movement.
