EPHEMERIS
Planetary positions are computed from JPL’s Keplerian elements for the planets (E. M. Standish, valid 1800–2050): each planet’s and Earth’s orbit is solved for the date, the Earth-to-planet direction gives the ecliptic longitude, and precession moves it to the equinox of date. The Moon uses a truncated lunar series. Checked against the astronomy-engine library across 2018–2030: within 0.08° for the planets and 0.32° for the Moon.
