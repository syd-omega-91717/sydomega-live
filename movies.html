FRANCHISE ARCHITECTURE
THE 12 FRANCHISES
Every sovereign film on Cinema is the anchor of a franchise: one Olympian, one sign, one element, plus its matching Chronicles series on Series. Movies is the index that ties both together into one saga per sign.
NOT A DUPLICATE OF CINEMA
Cinema lists the 12 films themselves. Series lists all 144 episodes across every track. Movies is the franchise-level view — what belongs to what, at a glance, before drilling into either.
GATE-LOCKED LIKE THE REST
Each franchise unlocks at the same gate as its anchor film. Franchise XII (Ocean Sovereign) is the apex saga, requiring AUTH = 27.8367 — the same apex credential as every other Gate XII surface on the platform.
