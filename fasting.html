METABOLIC PHASES OF FASTING
⚡
0–4 HRS: FED STATE
Insulin elevated. Glucose used as primary fuel. Fat burning minimal. Digestion active.
🔋
4–8 HRS: POST-ABSORPTIVE
Blood glucose normalizes. Liver glycogen begins depleting. Fat oxidation increases modestly.
🔥
8–12 HRS: FAT BURNING
Liver glycogen depleted. Fatty acid oxidation dominant. Ketone production begins. Mental clarity sharpens.
♻
12–16 HRS: KETOSIS ONSET
Blood ketones measurable. Autophagy upregulated. Insulin reaches lowest point. HGH begins rising.
🧬
16–24 HRS: DEEP KETOSIS
Autophagy markedly elevated (Yoshinori Ohsumi, 2016 Nobel Prize). Cellular cleanup peaks. Inflammation markers drop.
🛡
24–72 HRS: EXTENDED FAST
Stem cell regeneration (Longo, 2019). Immune system reset. Significant metabolic reprogramming. Medical supervision recommended beyond 48h.

RESEARCH FOUNDATIONS
AUTOPHAGY — Yoshinori Ohsumi (2016 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine)
Ohsumi's work established the molecular mechanisms of autophagy — the cellular process of self-cleaning where damaged organelles and proteins are broken down and recycled. Fasting, particularly beyond 16 hours, is among the most potent triggers of autophagy in humans. This process is implicated in cancer prevention, neurodegeneration protection, and longevity.
INTERMITTENT FASTING — Longo & Panda (2016) Cell Metabolism
Valter Longo and Satchidananda Panda's research established the metabolic benefits of time-restricted eating (TRE): improved insulin sensitivity, reduced inflammation, weight management, and circadian rhythm alignment. 16:8 TRE (16h fast, 8h eating window) produces measurable metabolic improvements without caloric restriction in otherwise healthy adults.
KETOSIS & COGNITIVE PERFORMANCE — Cahill (2006) Annual Review of Nutrition
George Cahill's landmark review established ketone bodies (specifically beta-hydroxybutyrate) as a superior fuel for the brain during fasting. BHB produces more ATP per unit of oxygen than glucose, and has neuroprotective properties. The cognitive clarity many fasters report after 12-16 hours reflects ketone-fueled brain metabolism.
HUMAN GROWTH HORMONE — Ho et al. (1988) Journal of Clinical Investigation
Ho's research showed that fasting stimulates HGH secretion by up to 5-fold within 24 hours. HGH promotes fat metabolism, muscle preservation, and cellular repair. This hormonal response partially explains the body-composition benefits of intermittent fasting independent of caloric intake.
FASTING-MIMICKING DIET — Longo et al. (2015) Cell Metabolism
Valter Longo's ProLon protocol (5-day fasting-mimicking diet, monthly) demonstrated reduction in risk factors for aging, diabetes, cardiovascular disease, and cancer in a randomized trial. Extended fasting triggers stem cell regeneration (Cheng et al. 2019, Cell Stem Cell) and immune system reset — mechanisms unavailable through daily caloric restriction.
