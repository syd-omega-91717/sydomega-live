THE 4-LAW FRAMEWORK (JAMES CLEAR)
1. Make it Obvious — Design your environment. Place triggers in sight.
2. Make it Attractive — Pair habits with things you enjoy.
3. Make it Easy — Reduce friction. 2-minute rule to start.
4. Make it Satisfying — Immediate reward. Track on a calendar.
THE 1% PRINCIPLE

1.01³⁶⁵ = 37.78

Small choices compound into sovereign outcomes.
HABIT STACKING FORMULA
"After I [CURRENT HABIT], I will [NEW HABIT]."

Link new behaviors to established anchors. The brain already has a strong neural pathway for the existing habit — piggyback on it.

Example: "After I pour my morning coffee, I will meditate for 2 minutes."
THE PLATEAU OF LATENT POTENTIAL
Habits often produce no visible results for weeks or months, then break through suddenly. This is the "valley of disappointment."

Most people quit during the plateau before reaching the breakthrough.

Trust the compound curve. The results are being stored — not lost.
IDENTITY-BASED HABITS
The most effective long-term habit formation comes from shifting your identity, not just your goals.

Instead of: "I want to exercise."
Say: "I am someone who trains daily."

Every habit vote is a vote for the person you wish to become.
ENVIRONMENT DESIGN
Motivation is overrated. Environment is architecture.

· Put books where you sit.
· Lay out workout clothes the night before.
· Remove the app from page 1 of your phone.

Make the default choice the right choice.
