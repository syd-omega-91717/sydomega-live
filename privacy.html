HOW LONG
The intended workflow deletes a document after a decision. Production deletion evidence and backup-retention behavior must be confirmed; this notice does not promise a fixed deletion deadline.
WITHDRAW
While your submission is pending you can withdraw it from your Passport. Withdrawal initiates deletion; the app should report failure and keep the item for owner follow-up until deletion succeeds.

SOVEREIGN PRIVACY ARCHITECTURE
ROW-LEVEL SECURITY
The platform uses PostgreSQL Row-Level Security to scope member records. Repository and runtime contracts verify the audited member-data tables; internal schemas and privileged functions are separately constrained. Do not infer that every internal table is exposed to members. Platform-owner authority is separately controlled through the platform_owners boundary.
CONSENT AS SOVEREIGN ACT
Consent choices are recorded in consent_records with timestamps, versions, and revocation records. Downstream enforcement for analytics and external providers is still being reconciled; do not assume every optional data flow is blocked solely by a consent toggle. Optional consent remains disabled when its status cannot be verified.
