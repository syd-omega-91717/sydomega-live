CINEMA ARCHITECTURE
THE 12 SOVEREIGN FILMS
Twelve films, one for each Olympian god and zodiac sign. Each film is gate-locked — viewing unlocks with authority. Film XII (Ocean Sovereign) requires Gate XII, AUTH = 27.8367 — the apex credential.
OLYMPIAN DIRECTORS
Each of the 12 Olympian gods directs one film, bound to their sign and element. The director's authority is expressed through the film's tone and structure — war epics for Ares, cerebral thrillers for Athena, ocean depths for Poseidon.
THE 9 ELEMENT TRACKS
Films are grouped by elemental affinity — Fire (I, X), Water (II, XII), Wind (III), Metal (VI, XI), Sand (IV), Soul (V), Space (VII), Void (VIII), The All (IX). Sand is the amplifier track, The All is the apex track.
SERIES EXPANSION
Each film spawns 12 series episodes, giving 144 total series entries. Series are extended universe content exploring the film's characters, elements, and lattice mechanics in greater depth. Gate-locked individually.
