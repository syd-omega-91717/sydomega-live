# Visual references (mood only) — not assets

Nine images were supplied as direction. They are **third-party works from commercial and stock sites** and are not
licensed for this repository: none is copied, embedded, traced or served. Production art is to be original, derived
from the written direction below. Two of the images show recognisable commercial games (a PlayStation store page for
*Dragon Age: The Veilguard* and a strategy-game screenshot), so designs must not imitate their distinctive characters,
silhouettes or logos.
