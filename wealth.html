COMPOUND INTEREST LAW
The eighth wonder of the world — money that earns money. A = P(1+r/n)^nt. Starting early multiplies wealth exponentially. Every dollar invested today is worth e^(r×t) in continuous compounding. The Rule of 72: divide 72 by your interest rate to find doubling time.
