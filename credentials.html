CREDENTIALS ARCHITECTURE
SOVEREIGN PASSPORT
The Sovereign Passport (the PASSPORT tab above) is a derived identity document generated from your profile data. Passport number is derived from your account UID. The passport reflects your current authority, gate, and element assignment.
CERTIFICATES OF ASCENT
Four certificates unlock progressively: Knowledge (Axis A ≥ 3), Mastery (Axis B ≥ 3), Contribution (Axis C ≥ 3), and Architect (Authority ≥ 13.92 / Gate VI). Each certificate is tied to your account record.
KYC PATHWAY
Know Your Customer verification follows four steps: email confirmation, platform approval, ID verification, and AML screening. Steps 1–2 are active; Steps 3–4 activate when the platform reaches that compliance tier.
BLOCKCHAIN IDENTITY
Your blockchain identity node is derived from your account UID and zodiac sign. Wallet activation is pending integration of the sovereign chain. Token assignment maps your sign to one of the 12 element tokens.
