### Zodiac Wars: Ascension, Slice 0 playable prototype (2026-11-28)

**What and why.** The studios master register listed the *Zodiac Wars: Ascension* game as DESIGNED only (a design document, "nothing built"), while the other three flagship games and the Two-Hand Bridge had playable prototypes. This builds Slice 0 exactly as the design document's section 6 scopes it: one arena (the Ares Foundry floor, Mars), two Houses (Aries: Kael Marren; Libra: Adaeze Okonkwo-Reyes), a training dummy, then one duel against the Aries Shadow, The Unbled. Status stays **PROTOTYPE**; the page says "not a released game" and that Zodiac Wars content is not founder-approved. Nothing is saved, there is no multiplayer, no network and no storage.

**Files.** `omega-zw-logic.js` (pure rules, UMD, fixed 60 Hz, driven only by an input log and a seed), `omega-zw-game.js` (Canvas 2-D screen, keyboard, gamepad and touch input, synthesised sound), `game-zodiac-wars.html`, `scripts/tests/test_zw_logic.py`, `scripts/verify-zw-game.js`. Wired into `nav.js` (MEDIA), `omega-guide.js`, `config/page-cores.json`, `config/page-world-overrides.json`, the `index.html` surface count and the generated skill registry.

**Rules implemented (design document section 3).** Health 100; light 8 damage in 18 frames; heavy 22 damage in 40 frames plus 12 recovery; guard blocks 70%; a perfect guard (first 6 frames) blocks 100% and opens the attacker for 20 frames; overreach +10 per hit dealt or taken, +25 for a heavy; at 100 the Shadow stance runs 8 s (damage x1.5, guard disabled) then a 3 s stagger; Aries pays up to 15 health into the next hit with the cost shown first; Libra counters a blocked hit (half the blocked damage returns, the attacker's overreach falls by 15, at a cost of 10 frames of tempo).

**Choices the document left open (marked CHOICE in the code).** The frame the button is pressed is frame 1 of an attack's frame data. One 1-D arena of 1000 units, no jump. Libra's counter is automatic on a non-perfect block. Overreach resets to 0 when the Shadow starts, and a hit counts only if it deals at least 1 damage. The Unbled follows the Shadow rules for its whole fight: 90 frames of intro, 8 s of pressure (x1.5, no guard), a 3 s stagger, repeat; its attack choices come from a seeded generator whose seed is on screen. The 9.1717 Hz canon number is the pulse of a 55 Hz drone, because a 9 Hz sine alone is below hearing.

**Verified.**
- `python3 -m unittest scripts.tests.test_zw_logic`: 15 tests OK. They assert every number above, that a scripted player beats the duel with both Houses and five seeds, that three duel replays equal their originals frame for frame (the design document's acceptance item), that the seed changes what the Unbled throws, that an idle player is beaten, and that the game files contain no network, payment, storage or `innerHTML` code.
- Mutation check: changing the guard block to 0.6, the perfect window to 3 frames, the counter drop to 5, or the Shadow multiplier to 1.2 each fails tests. A fifth mutant (the Unbled ignoring the seed) first **survived**, because the test compared a state hash that contains the seed number itself; the test now compares the Unbled's own attack sequence and that mutant is killed.
- A real bug the tests found: an attack's first frame was counted as frame 0, so "18 frames" ran 19. Fixed in the rules.
- `node scripts/verify-zw-game.js`: PASSED 23 of 23 in headless Chromium: the canvas is drawn at the screen's density and painted; real keyboard events move, attack and guard; touch buttons hold and release; every control is a real button; the scripted player, asked once per 60 Hz tick through the page's own loop, beats the dummy and the Unbled; the replay of that duel ends in the identical state; no horizontal overflow at 390 px and 44 px touch targets; reduced motion plays; no page errors. My first harness fed the player once per screen frame and lost the duel, because a six-frame perfect guard needs a decision every tick; that was the harness, and the page now has a small test hook (`OmegaZWGame.drive`) that nothing in the page sets.
- I looked at the screen: the knight and the Unbled (drawn as a jagged dark absence with an ember edge) read clearly, with a white arc on a perfect guard and the frame data and cost line visible.

**Not done / not verified (the design document's acceptance list is not complete).**
- **60 fps on a named mid-range device: not measured.** Only headless software rendering was run.
- **Not playtested by a human.** Every number is the document's, unplaytested. A scripted player that perfect-guards every strike loses no health, so the duel is trivially easy for it; a six-frame (100 ms) window may be very hard for people. Difficulty and feel are unknown.
- Figures are small against the arena (about 128 px tall in a 420 px frame); not yet re-proportioned.
- Not built from the document: full control remapping, IndexedDB saves with a checksum, the twelve gates read from `OmegaCanon`, the thirteenth seat, the Unbled's absence-shaped art beyond a first pass, other Houses and guardian assists. The document's open founder questions (nine proposed worlds, two versus three Houses, tactical multiplayer) are untouched.
- `docs/studios/OMEGA_STUDIOS_MASTER_STATUS.md` has a new row; the owner-only Studio Library payload (a server function) is not changed, so the game is not listed there.
