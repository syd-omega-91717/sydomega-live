LEARN THE REAL CONCEPTS
EARNING

In real crypto, tokens are "mined" or "earned" through proof of work, staking, or contribution. Here, you earn points through real proof of work too -- completing Academy lessons, clearing Gaming stages, and passing Exam Hall certifications.

WALLETS & BALANCE

A real wallet doesn't "hold" coins -- it holds keys that prove your claim to a balance recorded on a public ledger. Your balance card above works the same way: it's a live sum of every real ledger entry tied to your account.

PEER-TO-PEER TRADING

Real crypto trades are proposals that must be accepted before anything moves -- exactly like the trade simulator below. Nothing transfers until both sides confirm, and every trade is recorded permanently.

SPENDING & UTILITY

A token's "utility" is what it can actually be redeemed for within its own system. Your points' utility is real internal perks -- themes, priority processing, profile flourishes -- not external money, which is exactly the distinction that keeps a token from being treated as a security.
