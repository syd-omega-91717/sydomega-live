### Sign stories: 12 constellation myths that open chapter by chapter as the member really progresses (2026-11-15)

**Why.** The platform is built on the 12 signs, gods and agents, and every member is one of the 12 characters, but nothing told the member *their* story. The owner asked for stories from my own knowledge rather than waiting to be given them. The classical constellation myths are public domain and map directly onto the canon.

**What.** `omega-stories.json` (new, root, data only): for each of the 12 signs, 4 chapters written for this platform: **I The Myth** (my retelling of the classical story), **II The Trial** (what the sign asks of the member, naming the canon agent), **III The Patron** (the canon god, "in this world"), **IV The Star** (an astronomical fact and the canon gate). Plus `sources` (Apollodorus, Hyginus, Ovid, Eratosthenes, Aratus, Homer, Pindar...) and a `variant` field naming where traditions differ. 48 chapters, about 2,200 words. `omega-character-card.js` gains `renderStory` and a `[data-omega-character-story]` mount: chapter k opens when the member has really passed `chapter_gates[k]` authority gates (`[0, 2, 5, 9]`, chapter I open to everyone), using the same arithmetic as the card; **a locked chapter shows the gate it needs and its text is never put in the page**. Mounted under the character card on the IDENTITY tab of `character.html`. The file holds stories only: gods, agents and gates are read from `omega-canon.json`, and `test_character_card.py` fails if a chapter does not name the canon's own agent (II), god (III) and gate (IV) for its sign, so the story cannot drift from the canon (§8.1 class 8).

**Accuracy choices (checked against my knowledge of the sources, no live source lookup).** Where the ancient record is not one story I tell one and say so in `variant`: Sagittarius is told as Chiron, but the best-attested ancient identification is Crotus the satyr (Chiron is the later popular one and has his own constellation, Centaurus); Virgo follows Aratus's Astraea; Libra is told as the Romans' scales, noting the Greek "claws of the Scorpion"; Capricorn follows Pan, not Amalthea; Cancer's crab is a minor figure in the oldest telling. The pairing of each sign with its god and agent is the platform's own canon, not tradition, and chapter III says "in this world". Astronomy lines are common facts (Hamal, Aldebaran and the Pleiades, Castor and Pollux, Praesepe, Regulus, Spica, Zubenelgenubi/Zubeneschamali, Antares, the galactic centre in Sagittarius, Fomalhaut, Alrescha).
