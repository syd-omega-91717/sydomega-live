### hi-DPI wave 4: the remaining canvas files, converted where drawing at the pixel ratio is worth its cost (2026-11-24)

**Why.** The owner asked for the remaining canvas files after the pointer-driven group. Audit on `origin/main` at `dc753cbf7` (a `grep` for `getContext('2d')` in files with no `devicePixelRatio`): **48** files. Each canvas was read, not assumed, and they split three ways. This entry says which, with the reason, because "extend to every file" was not the right action for all of them.

**Converted (17 files).**
- *14 pages, 22 canvases, through the page helper:* `ascension`, `body` (2), `beacon`, `breath`, `clarity`, `houses`, `lab`, `nutrition` (2 charts plus the macro rings), `skills`, `sleep` (4), `stoic` (2), `sigil` (2, plus its gallery canvases), `vision`, `focus` (3).
- *3 shared modules, each with its own capped ratio maths* because `bg.js` injects them on every page and a page-level helper cannot be assumed: `emblem.js` (the topbar emblem on every page), `omega-element-motif.js` (cosmos, elements), `omega-lattice-3d.js` (the 3-D lattice modal).

**Already drawn at about 2x by hand (21 files; 5 of them, marked *, also have a backdrop below), left as they are.** Read from the markup: the store is twice the CSS box (`width="120" style="width:60px"`, `cv.width=W*2` with `style.width=W+'px'`, `ctx.scale(2,2)`). They are `analytics` (mini-radar, apex), `automation`, `identity`, `points`, `social`, `subscriptions`, `triads`, `trophies`, `vault`, `interface-omni`, `city`, `cosmos`, `family`, `media`*, `profile`*, `honors`*, `enter`*, `pending`*, `nav.js` (the sidebar brand), `approvals-ui.js`, `omega-page-emblem.js`. Not measured in a browser. At a ratio of 3 they are upscaled 1.5x.

**Deliberately NOT converted, with the reason.** In all, 31 of the 48 files are left alone (16 hand-doubled only, 5 hand-doubled with a backdrop, 5 backdrop only, 2 outputs, 3 not display canvases); 17 are converted.
- *Decorative full-viewport backdrops, 10 files* (`404`, `account`, `offline`, and the background layers of `honors`, `media`, `profile` (incl. its hero particle canvas), `enter`, `pending`, plus `omega-confetti.js` and `OmegaParticles` in `omega-animated.js`). A phone viewport of 393x852 CSS px at ratio 3 is 1179x2556 = 3.0 M pixels (12 MB) against 0.33 M (1.3 MB) at 1x, redrawn every frame, for scattered dots and hairlines nobody inspects. **Arithmetic only: I did not measure frame time**, and software Chromium would not represent a phone GPU. This is a judgement, not a finding; a capped ratio of 1.5 or 2 is a one-line change per backdrop if the owner prefers it.
- *Canvases whose pixel size is the output, 2 files* (`omega-qr.js`, `omega-share-card.js`): scaling them changes the file a member downloads or shares.
- *Not display canvases, 3 files:* `omega-alive.js` (a 12 px probe that measures whether a glyph renders as emoji), `omega-page-features.js` (feature detection), `omega-graphify.js` (never sets its canvas size; its page does).

**Fix.** `omega-canvas-hidpi.js` gained `fitAttr(canvas)` for attribute-sized canvases: the logical size is read once from the attributes (they hold the larger store afterwards); it is safe on every animation frame because it does not reassign width when the store already matches; and above 1x the store follows the **displayed** width, because a `width:100%` chart whose attribute is 700 but whose box is 778 px was already stretched 11 percent at 1x. At 1x nothing changes. `fit()` gained a `scale` option, and its transform is now the real store/CSS ratio.

**Verified.** `verify-hidpi-charts.js` now covers every wave: **342/342** (it also re-checked all the earlier pages after the helper change). `verify-hidpi-pointer.js` **80/80** (atlas still not verified). New `verify-hidpi-modules.js`: **23/23** at 1x, 2.625x and 3x (CSS box unchanged, store exactly logical x ratio, not blank, painted region correct). Mutations caught: removing the transform in `emblem.js` and in `omega-lattice-3d.js` each fail; a `fitAttr` page reading `cv.width` back as its size fails `test_a_page_that_uses_fit_attr_never_reads_the_store_back_as_its_size`.

**Not done / not verified.** (1) The hand-doubled files and the files left alone are not browser-measured. (2) `clarity`'s ring and `vision`'s wheel were exercised by loading and opening the tab; the other session-driven draws of those pages were not driven. (3) `sleep` and `nutrition` were drawn from seeded data through their draw functions or at load, not through their save forms. (4) Software Chromium only; no real device. (5) Module canvases drawn at 3x on a page with many emblems add memory: one 64 px emblem is 192x192 = 147 KB, not measured against the whole estate. (6) Not deployed.
