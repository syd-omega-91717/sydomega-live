DEEP WORK (CAL NEWPORT) — Professional activity performed in a state of distraction-free concentration that pushes cognitive capabilities to the limit. These efforts create new value, improve skill, and are hard to replicate.

ULTRADIAN RHYTHMS — The brain operates in 90-minute rest-activity cycles (ultradian cycles). The optimal focus session aligns to exactly 90 minutes of deep work followed by 20 minutes of true rest.

THE 4-HOUR RULE — Research (Ericsson, Newport) consistently shows elite performers rarely sustain more than 4 hours of genuine deep work per day. Distribute sessions in the morning when prefrontal cortex capacity peaks.

FLOW STATE TRIGGERS — Clear goals, immediate feedback, challenge-to-skill ratio of ~4% above current ability, no interruptions for first 15 minutes.
