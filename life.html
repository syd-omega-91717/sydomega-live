THE PRIVACY CONTRACT

Your records are private to your authenticated account. AI processing is off by default and requires both account-level and item-level permission. Highly sensitive items require separate consent. This page itself does not call an AI provider or send your content to web services.

Do not enter another person’s sensitive information unless you have a lawful basis and appropriate permission. AI analysis, web research and proactive alerts remain inactive unless their separately governed integrations are configured.
