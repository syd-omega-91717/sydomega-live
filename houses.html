01
JUNGIAN ARCHETYPES
Jung's archetypal framework maps closely to astrological houses. The 12 houses represent the 12 primary life arenas — Self, Resources, Communication, Home, Creativity, Service, Partnership, Transformation, Wisdom, Career, Community, and Transcendence.
02
ANGULAR VS CADENT HOUSES
Angular houses (1,4,7,10) are strongest, linked to action and external manifestation. Succedent (2,5,8,11) consolidate. Cadent (3,6,9,12) process and transition. This distinction comes from Hellenistic astrology and the concept of "places of power."
03
HEMISPHERIC ANALYSIS
Southern hemisphere (7-12) = public, relationship-oriented domains. Northern (1-6) = private, self-development domains. Eastern (10-3) = self-directed. Western (4-9) = other-directed. Hemisphere concentration reveals fundamental life orientation.
04
HOUSE RULERS
Each house is ruled by a planet based on its natural sign. The ruler's position reveals where energy flows from that house — a planet ruling the 2nd house of wealth in the 10th house of career suggests wealth tied to professional reputation.
05
INTERCEPTED HOUSES
In whole-sign and Placidus systems, some signs are "intercepted" — entirely contained within a house. These represent repressed or unconscious domains requiring conscious development. A common finding in psychological astrology.
06
THE SOVEREIGN LATTICE
The SYD OMEGA 12-house lattice maps each house to a strategic life domain: Authority, Wealth, Intelligence, Heritage, Creation, Service, Alliance, Transformation, Wisdom, Legacy, Network, and Mystery. A sovereign governs all 12.
