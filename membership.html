MEMBERSHIP ARCHITECTURE
THE AUTHORITY FORMULA
AUTH = sqrt(A³ + B³ + C³) × φ / e, where φ = 1.6180339887 (golden ratio) and e = 2.7182818285 (Euler's number). The three axes — Knowledge (A), Mastery (B), and Contribution (C) — each scale from 0.001 to 9. Full APEX requires all three axes at their maximum: AUTH = 27.8367.
THE TWELVE GATES
The 12 Gates correspond to thresholds in the authority score: Gate I at 2.32, ascending through Gate XII at 27.8367 (APEX). Each gate unlocks further platform capability. The GNAMES sequence — INITIATE → ACOLYTE → SCHOLAR → KEEPER → GUARDIAN → ARCHITECT → SOVEREIGN → VANGUARD → HERALD → ORACLE → PRIME → APEX SOVEREIGN — marks the journey.
TIER DISTINCTION
Three active tiers exist above Trial: Standard (approved membership, all gates accessible), Prime (AUTH ≥ 27.10, Gate XI+), and Apex Sovereign (AUTH = 27.8367, Gate XII). Tier determines benefit access, certificate eligibility, and governance rights within the Order. The tier guard runs client-side against the member profile fetched at boot.
TRIAL PERIOD
Prospective members enter a 7-day trial. Trial accounts see the dashboard and character profile but cannot access gates II–XII, cinema, or the marketplace. When the trial expires, the member is redirected to the pending page. Full access requires approval by the platform owner via the Approvals module.
