### Catalogue status stated once per section on gaming and academy (2026-11-05)

**Why.** Measured 2026-11-05: 236 pages, 78,504 visible words, median 280. The two heaviest pages were `gaming.html` (2,816) and `academy.html` (2,214), and most of that was one label repeated: 132 × "CATALOGUE · NOT HOSTED" plus a per-card axis tag and category line on `gaming.html`, 96 × "CATALOGUE · TEST IN ACADEMY" plus a per-card category tag on `academy.html`. Every card in a section carried the same category, axis tag and status as the section header above it. The labels exist because of the owner's 2026-10-18 decision (a catalogue card must not read as playable or as a sat exam), so they cannot simply go.

**Fix.** The status moves into each section header (11 on gaming, 8 on academy). On `gaming.html` the 72 game cards lose category, axis tag and status; the 60 exam cards keep only `LEVEL N`. On `academy.html` each exam card keeps `LEVEL N · AXIS X` (the axis differs per card in places, e.g. Professional's cards are AXIS B under an AXIS A header) and loses the repeated category tag and status. Three dead CSS rules went from `gaming.html`, one from `academy.html`. `academy.html` keeps an empty `.ec-meta` per card: `markDone()` appends "RECORDED" into it and silently skips when it is missing. `scripts/tests/test_catalogue_status_once.py` pins all of this.
