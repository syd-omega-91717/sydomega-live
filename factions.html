THE TWELVE HOUSES
Twelve Factions divide the Order — one sovereign house per sign, bound to its element and its Olympian. Your sign sets your Faction; it is your banner among the sovereigns.
CELESTIAL KNIGHTS — THE ZODIAC WARS
The Celestial Knights are the champions of each Faction in the Zodiac Wars — the great conflict that shaped the Sovereign Order. Each Knight carries a unique role in the Order's founding narrative.
FACTION ARCHITECTURE
TWELVE HOUSES
The 12 Factions map directly to the 12 zodiac signs, each aligned with an element (Fire, Water, Wind, Metal, Sand) and an Olympian deity. Faction membership is determined by the member's sovereign sign — set in their profile cosmology. Faction is immutable once set.
ELEMENT BINDING
Each element governs a domain of the Sovereign Matrix: Fire governs the Dojo and competitive mastery; Water governs flow and contribution; Wind governs knowledge and speed; Metal governs trade and precision; Sand governs patience and foundation. Your element determines your natural axis of progression.
CELESTIAL KNIGHTS
Each Faction's Celestial Knight is the protagonist of that sign's Zodiac Wars narrative — the canonical conflict that defined the sovereign order. Knights are not users; they are the Order's founding archetypes, recorded in the Sovereign Codex as permanent reference points for each sign's potential.
THE SOVEREIGN CREED
Each Faction has a Creed — a single sovereign principle that defines how that house approaches power, knowledge, and contribution. The Creed is a compass, not a rule. It calibrates how members of that sign are expected to operate within the 9×9×9 Matrix.
