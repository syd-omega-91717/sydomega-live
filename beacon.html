THE BEACON SYSTEM
THREE AXES, ONE PATH
The Order is not organised by seniority or wealth. It is organised by three axes: Knowledge (A), Mastery (B), and Contribution (C). Every action you take on the platform advances one or more axes. Authority is the geometric sum: AUTH = √(A³+B³+C³) × φ/e.
THE 729-NODE LATTICE
12 phases × 12 levels × 12 signs = 1,728 coordinates. The inner sovereign cube is 9×9×9 = 729 nodes. Every member occupies a position in this lattice. Your position is determined by your authority score relative to the APEX of 27.8367.
COSMOLOGY AS IDENTITY
Your zodiac sign is not decorative — it assigns you to one of the twelve factions and maps you to an Olympian god, a Roman god, an Egyptian deity, and a Hindu Navagraha. The cross-pantheon mapping is the identity layer that connects you to all four canonical traditions.
THE BEACON AS PROTOCOL
The Beacon is the orientation protocol for new members. It checks your progress against six onboarding milestones, renders your ring completion, and maps the Order's architecture so you know where you are and where to go. It resets each year as the Order evolves.
