WELLNESS ARCHITECTURE
THE NINTH ELEMENT
The Ninth Element layer covers the 10 life domains: Religion, Character, Mind, Heart, Birth, Death, Life, Family, Friends, Work. Each domain has a wellness dimension. Health data feeds directly into the matrix — maintaining your biological sovereignty is part of your ascension path.
MASTERY AXIS (B)
Each wellness log fires an OmegaProgress event that advances the Mastery axis (B). This is the discipline axis — consistent daily logging demonstrates the sovereign discipline required to maintain peak frequency. The axis advance is recorded in the health_logs table and reflected on your profile ring.
THE 5 DIMENSIONS
Mind, Heart, Energy, Body, and Soul form the five wellness pillars. Each is scored 1–9 (matching the sovereign matrix scale). The Sovereign Score is the mean of all five, providing a single at-a-glance frequency rating. Scores are averaged across the last 14 entries for display.
DATA SOVEREIGNTY
All health logs are stored in the Supabase health_logs table, protected by Row Level Security. Only the authenticated member can read their own logs. No health data is shared, aggregated, or used for any purpose outside the member's personal wellness dashboard.
