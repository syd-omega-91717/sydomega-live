HOW THE ATLAS BECOMES REAL

1 · OBJECT

Every place is a typed object with stable identity, parent, provenance and truth state.

2 · CAPABILITY

Every city/district service resolves to an existing governed module or registered capability.

3 · MISSION

Tasks become mission definitions and member progress, never decorative buttons.

4 · EVIDENCE

Actions emit durable events and evidence so achievements and progress remain provable.

5 · GOVERNANCE

Country/empire/city policies are simulation rules until real legal/provider evidence exists.

6 · 3-D
