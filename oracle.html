MOON PHASE CALCULATION
The synodic period of the Moon is 29.53 days. The oracle calculates the current phase by measuring elapsed days from the known new moon of January 6, 2000 (18:14 UTC) and dividing by the synodic cycle modulo 8 phase bins: New, Waxing Crescent, First Quarter, Waxing Gibbous, Full, Waning Gibbous, Last Quarter, Waning Crescent.

ORBITAL PERIOD COMPUTATION
Planetary positions are computed deterministically from the J2000 epoch (January 1, 2000) using known orbital periods: Sun 365.25d · Moon 29.53d · Mercury 87.97d · Venus 224.7d · Mars 686.97d · Jupiter 4332.59d · Saturn 10759.22d · Uranus 30688.5d · Neptune 60182d · Pluto 90560d. Each planet's zodiac sign is derived from its fractional orbit position modulo 12.
