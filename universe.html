UNIVERSE ARCHITECTURE
THE 12-FOLD LATTICE
The SYD OMEGA universe is built on a 12-fold structure — 12 signs, 12 factions, 12 cities, 12 chapters per narrative arc. Every content piece maps to at least one sign, one element, and one gate threshold.
GATE-LOCKED CONTENT
Cinematic and gaming content is progressively unlocked as members advance through the 12 gates. Gate I content is available to all approved members; Gate XII content requires APEX authority (27.8367).
SIX CONTENT PILLARS
Cinema, Series, Gaming, Characters, Cosmos, and Lore form the six content pillars. Each pillar maps to one or more axes: Cinema → Axis C Contribution; Gaming → Axis B Mastery; Lore → Axis A Knowledge.
CANONICAL STATUS
All titles are catalogued as IN DEVELOPMENT or CONCEPT. Development timelines are tied to platform milestones — member growth, gate distribution, and Sovereign Order consensus unlock production phases.
