HALL ARCHITECTURE
THE LIVING RECORD
The Hall of the Order is a real-time record of every approved sovereign member. Statistics are pulled live from the database via the order_stats RPC — member count, certificate count, and evolution events are always current.
AUTHORITY RANKING
Members are ranked by authority score (AUTH = √(A³+B³+C³) × φ/e). The leaderboard shows the top 25 ascendants. Owners are always sovereign-tagged and displayed with their canonical authority regardless of axes.
ELEMENT DISTRIBUTION
The five elements (Fire, Water, Wind, Metal, Sand) are distributed by sovereign sign. Distribution reflects which signs are most represented in the current member base — a live snapshot of the Order's elemental balance.
PRIVACY
Only names, signs, elements, and authority scores appear in the Hall. Private profile data (email, axes detail, asset holdings) is never exposed. Members who have opted into portfolio visibility may appear with expanded data in future cycles.
