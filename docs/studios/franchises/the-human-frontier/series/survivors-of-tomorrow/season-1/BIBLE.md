## Premise
A run of planetary crises has left one walled city, the Last City, with eleven thousand people and a ledger. Season 1 is a civic drama about arithmetic:
what a community owes its members when it cannot afford to be honest, and who decides when the numbers are allowed to be public. It sets up the film *The
Last City*, where survivors choose between the old order and something new.

## Method
Every episode is built around one **public number**: a ration, a water level, a vote count. The number is true; what changes is who is allowed to say it.
Survival is real, but nobody is killed by a monster. The season's antagonist is a good reason kept too long.

## Continuity locks
1. The city is called the Last City; it is not claimed to be the last city anywhere, only the last the characters know of.
2. Reserves are measured in days of water and in rations, never in a vague "supplies".
3. No character is a soldier; there is a guard, and it follows the Warden's orders until episode 105.
4. Nothing links this franchise to the Zodiac Wars, the Stones or the Omega signal. Any rhyme is for the Convergence to earn.

## Episode guide (8)
- 101 **Ration Day** — the ration is cut 12%; Ines finds the reserve figure is wrong.
- 102 **The Posted Number** — the city reads the true figure and does not riot, which frightens the Warden more.
- 103 **East Wall** — Tobias's repair does not hold; the cost is stated aloud.
- 104 **The Old Order** — the Ledger's founders, remembered by the people who liked them.
- 105 **The Guard** — the guard is asked to choose between the Warden and the city.
- 106 **Two Hundred Votes** — the first vote; most people do not come.
- 107 **What the Warden Counted** — Osei opens the oldest page of the ledger.
- 108 **A Season Reborn** — the vote is taken again; the city decides what to do with the number.
