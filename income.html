Calculation definitions

Gross billings: projected paying members × assumed monthly price.

Churn: rounded percentage of the opening member count, applied before adding new paying members.

Refund estimate: gross billings × assumed refund percentage.

Tax estimate: post-refund billings × user-entered tax assumption. This is not a jurisdictional tax calculation.

Processing estimate: gross billings × assumed percentage plus assumed fixed fee × paying members.

Net operating cash estimate: gross billings − refund estimate − tax estimate − processing estimate − operating cost. It excludes chargebacks, discounts, failed collections, FX, reserves, payout delays, capex, payroll, financing and corporate tax.
