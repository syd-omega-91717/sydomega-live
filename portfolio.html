PORTFOLIO ARCHITECTURE
AUTHORITY FORMULA
AUTH = √(A³+B³+C³) × φ/e — where A=Knowledge, B=Mastery, C=Contribution (each 0–9). APEX = 27.8367. φ=1.6180339887, e=2.7182818285. Authority is the sovereign measure of your position across all three axes of the 9×9×9 Matrix.
THREE AXES
Axis A (Knowledge) advances through study, research, and verified output. Axis B (Mastery) advances through skill trials, dojo performance, and discipline logs. Axis C (Contribution) advances through missions, peer rating, and published work. Each axis is scored 0–9.
SOVEREIGN ASSETS
Holdings represent your stake in the Sovereign Order — tokens, collectibles, credentials, and domain resources. Assets are recorded in the user_assets ledger and updated by mission outcomes, trade, and sovereign grants.
PRIVACY CONTROLS
Your portfolio is private by default. Enabling the public toggle shares your authority score, axis levels, and achievement count with the Order. Holdings and cosmology details remain visible only to you.
