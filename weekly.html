THE WEEKLY REVIEW — DAVID ALLEN
David Allen's GTD system identifies the weekly review as the cornerstone of the entire productivity system. Without a consistent review practice, your mind cannot trust the system — it starts re-processing commitments, creating anxiety and cognitive overhead. The weekly review clears the mental inbox, updates project lists, and closes open loops that otherwise drain attention continuously.
REFLECTION & DELIBERATE PRACTICE
K. Anders Ericsson's deliberate practice research shows that experts consistently outperform others not because of more practice, but because of more structured feedback and reflection. A weekly review creates a feedback loop: what worked, what failed, and why. Without systematic reflection, improvement is random rather than directed. Sovereign growth requires knowing not just what you did, but what it cost and what it produced.
