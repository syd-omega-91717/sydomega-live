EVOLUTION ARCHITECTURE
THREE AXES
Authority is composed of three axes: A (Knowledge), B (Mastery), C (Contribution). Each axis ranges from 0 to 9. Authority = √(A³+B³+C³) × φ/e. Apex authority of 27.8367 is achieved at (9,9,9).
12 GATES
Authority unlocks 12 gates at thresholds from 2.32 (INITIATE) to 27.8367 (APEX SOVEREIGN). Each gate unlocks platform features, NFT lines, and sovereign privileges.
EVOLUTION EVENTS
Every axis update, task completion, or authority milestone is recorded as an evolution event. Events are stored in the evolution_events table and shown in real-time in the Events tab.
PROGRESSION STRATEGY
Balanced axis growth produces faster authority gains due to the cubic formula. A single axis at 9 with the others at 0 yields AUTH ≈ 10.55. Balanced (3,3,3) yields AUTH ≈ 9.15. Full (9,9,9) yields APEX 27.8367.
