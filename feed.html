SOVEREIGN COMMUNICATION LAYER
ACTIVITY AS EVIDENCE
The activity feed is not social media. Every entry in the feed is a recorded matrix action — a task completed, a gate crossed, a certificate earned. Activity is the proof of sovereignty, not the performance of it.
AXIS ATTRIBUTION
Every action in the platform is tagged to an axis: A (Knowledge), B (Mastery), or C (Contribution). The feed reflects this tagging. Watching the feed reveals where the Order's collective energy flows and which axes are being built fastest.
PUBLICATIONS AS PROOF
Sovereign posts are not social updates. Each publication is a piece of sovereign intelligence — a report, essay, or analysis submitted to the Order's knowledge base. Publications advance Axis A and earn certificates when reviewed and approved.
PLATFORM ARCHITECTURE
The activity stream reads from the task_completions table via Supabase Realtime. The feed is live and RLS-protected: platform owners see all activity; members see public records only. No user data is exposed beyond what the profile owner has approved.
