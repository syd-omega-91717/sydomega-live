## Logline
When the Solar Nexus wakes after twelve thousand years, a Mars foundry apprentice who cannot stop counting what things cost
must reach the thirteenth seat before the man who taught him to stop.

## Classification and audience (proposed)
Genre: epic science-fantasy drama. Audience: 12+. Content notes: war violence (one on-screen killing, bloodless in the
Shadows' case by design), grief, no strong language, no sexual content. Running time target: 110 minutes once expanded.

## Three-act structure
- **Act I (Mars, Earth, Neptune).** The foundry's oath ("say the cost before the pour"). Kael hears 9.1717 Hz in the floor. The Nexus wakes; every Accord bell rings unstruck. Ossara raids the foundry, kills his old master Orla Tevane and takes the Aries tongue; Kael hides in the casting pit and survives. Ares appoints him Knight. He burns for Earth.
- **Act II (Earth).** Kael is held by Mediator Adaeze Okonkwo-Reyes, freed by his ship's own testimony, and learns Ossara petitions to ring the tongue under Accord law. Three Light rulers brand three Knights (Aries, Libra, Pisces). Each Shadow tempts its Knight overnight. The Trial of Cost ends in a tie; Adaeze rules for the law's reason over its letter; Ossara answers with a legal coup. Midpoint: Kael strikes in anger and is stopped by Adaeze's sanctuary clause. Lowest point: Adaeze jailed, Solenne forgets Ima. Ima recites the old story's last line, which is the key; Adaeze appeals to the people, who consent to open the Hall.
- **Act III (the Nexus).** Ima returns Solenne's memory; Solenne defeats the Drowned by naming the living. Adaeze amends the law and the Sworn dissolves. Kael makes Ossara read his forty thousand names; the Unbled bleeds for the first time. The Null speaks, offering each Knight relief from responsibility; only Kael refuses aloud, and the others hold. Ossara surrenders. Kael rings the new Aries bell at the foundry. Tag: the door of Soul opens a hand's width. Post-credit: the Unbled's wound drips.

## Locations
Ares Foundry, Olympus Ridge, Mars; Meridian City and the Balance Court's Hall of the Seats, Earth; the Deep Archive, Neptune; the Nexus (nine rings, four doors, one bell-shaped socket).

## Visual, sound and effects direction
Mars: red furnace light, bronze and soot. Earth: pale stone and morning daylight, ordinary and civic, so the Nexus feels uncanny by contrast. Neptune: blue water-light, glass tanks. The Shadows are absences drawn with an ember edge; the Unbled's wounds must never drip until the end. The 9.1717 Hz hum is the film's score spine: begin as a single sub-audible tone, split into harmonics as the Nexus wakes, resolve to one pure stroke at the climax. Required effects: unstruck bells, the sky seam, three elemental god-presences (fire, water, silver light), the Shadows, the Nexus rings and doors, the Null as a fold in the air. Practical-first is recommended for the foundry.
