CHARACTER ARCHITECTURE

AUTHORITY FORMULA
AUTH = √(A³+B³+C³) × φ/e where φ=1.6180339887 and e=2.7182818285. Maximum is APEX=27.8367 when A=B=C=9. The formula is irreversible — you rise by earning, not by declaration.
THE 9 ELEMENTS
Three triads: Primal (Fire, Water, Wind), Middle (Metal, Sand, Soul), Supreme (Space, Void, The All). Each element maps to a zodiac sign, an Olympian deity, a sovereign gate, and a lattice track. The 9th element — The All — is held only by Apex sovereigns.
SIGN CROSS-REFERENCE
Each of the 12 zodiac signs maps to one Olympian deity, one element, and one sovereign gate. The gate determines your entry point into the lattice — not your rank, but your origin frequency. Your rank is always a function of your axis values.
TOKEN ECONOMY
The 9-element token system underpins the sovereign economy. Each element token is issued when the corresponding gate threshold is crossed. Omega tokens (The All element) are reserved for those who have reached APEX authority — AUTH ≥ 27.8367.
