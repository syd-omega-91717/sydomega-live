## Logline
When an archaeologist unearths an ancient stone, a descendant of an extinct order of guardians learns that the seven legendary
artifacts are not weapons but the parts of a mechanism built to stop any one hand from owning reality, and that his own
ancestors broke it.

## Classification and audience (proposed)
Epic fantasy, mystery, adventure. Audience 12+. Content notes: peril and sedation violence, bereavement, one on-screen
catastrophe (the Sundering) without gore. No strong language. Target runtime 125-140 minutes once expanded.

## Three-act structure
- **Act I.** The Aurelith's last day: the Conclave tries to bind the seven stones to one throne; the seven Guardians take them from the Loom and the sky tears (the Sundering). Malachar, who will not give up his stone, is stunned by Ithren and hidden. Four thousand years later Dr Lyra Thorne lifts the Memory stone from a buried temple; at the same moment Auren Vale has the first vision. A raid scatters the camp; Auren pauses time for two seconds. Seraphine's bookshop explains the seven aspects. Malachar tells his lieutenant: "I want it back."
- **Act II.** The Guardian Citadel (Auren's face on Ithren's statue; Malachar was Ithren's friend). The Crystal Depths: a two-key lock where "no one hand holds the whole"; Malachar appears and shows Auren the Aurelith through his eyes, then lets them keep the Matter stone. Midpoint: Malachar is sympathetic and warns them about Seraphine. The Kingdom of Ash: Creation tempts Auren with his mother; Torin, raised by Malachar, drugs the team and takes three stones. Seraphine confesses she is Eryndor, and that the Guardians built the Loom as a throne for themselves. The sky begins to show a ghost city.
- **Act III.** At the Eternal Temple Eryndor and Malachar meet and she tells him the truth. He tries to make the Loom answer him; it shows a child who can only repeat one sentence. The Loom offers Auren the throne (his mother); he refuses. Seven people, including two of Malachar's side, release seven stones into the lattice at once. Malachar stays to release Time last; his fate is unknown. Final scene: twelve sky-signs light and a voice says the houses must prepare. Post-credit: a sealed archive map of seven worlds and twelve territories centred on the Omega symbol.

Proposed additions, all registered in `config/omega-studio-registry.json`: Ithren, four other Guardians, Speaker Verran, Liora, Isolde Vale, Hollis Reeve, Tuan Pham, Vesper Lund, Corvin Hale, Nell, The Lamp.

## Locations (each tied to the game's regions)
The Aurelith and the Loom chamber; Kharesh Basin and its buried temple; Port Vessa (museum lab, bookshop); the Guardian Citadel; the Crystal Depths; the Kingdom of Ash (Karth Vael); the Eternal Temple and its vault.

## Visual, sound and effects direction
The Aurelith: white stone, uphill canals, seven coloured lights in a ring. The present day is grounded and dusty so that the stones' effects land. Time-slip is a sustained freeze with one live element (Auren). The 9.1717 Hz hum is the score spine: a single low tone, splitting into seven pure notes at the release. Required effects: the sky seam, time-stopped dust and dart, living crystal architecture, a city of self-rebuilding ash, a ghost city that descends to the temple. **Design guardrail:** a supplied reference shows colour-coded gems floating around an altar; the stones here are not to be staged that way. Each stone is a distinct material (grey water-glass, amber, brick, green glass, rose-gold, clear, cobalt) sitting in a cradle of a two-key lattice.
