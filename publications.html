PUBLICATION SCIENCE
01
READING & INTELLIGENCE
Reading 30+ books per year is correlated with the top 1% of knowledge workers. Bill Gates reads 50 books/year. Warren Buffett attributes 80% of his learning to reading. For the sovereign, systematic reading is a primary intellectual leverage point.
02
DEEP READING VS SKIMMING
Maryanne Wolf's "Reader Come Home": deep reading activates analogical reasoning, empathy, and critical reflection. Skimming activates different neural pathways. The sovereign reads deeply on primary subjects and strategically skims for breadth.
03
ANTILIBRARY CONCEPT
Nassim Taleb's antilibrary: the books you haven't read are more valuable than the ones you have. An antilibrary is a reminder of what you don't know. The sovereign keeps a large, categorized queue of unread works as a humility and growth signal.
04
PUBLISHING AS AUTHORITY
Published works — books, papers, essays — crystallize and broadcast your ideas at scale. Writing clarifies thinking (Zinsser), creates artifacts others engage with, and compounds authority far beyond individual conversations.
05
CITATION IMPACT
Academic citation analysis: a single highly-cited paper shapes thousands of subsequent works. The sovereign's writing aspires to the same: ideas that seed other ideas across time. Track the downstream reach of your intellectual contributions.
06
THE READING ELITE
Studies consistently show that society's highest-performing individuals share systematic, diverse reading habits. Naval Ravikant: "I don't read to finish books; I read to find things that change my mental models." Reading for transformation, not completion.
