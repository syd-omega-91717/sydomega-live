### elements.html motif canvases: art no longer stretched 1.4x sideways (2026-11-28)

**How it was found.** A sweep of every canvas on the 143 pages that contain one (`main` at `548e6fd1d`, 1000px wide, device pixel ratio 3, 2.2 s after load; 2198 canvases, 1269 visible, 929 not visible and therefore NOT measured). It flags a visible canvas whose backing-store aspect differs from its displayed box. After removing the ones that were not actually drawn at sweep time, exactly one page has real, visible distortion.

**Why.** On `elements.html` each of the nine cards draws its motif (`omega-element-motif.js`) on a canvas authored 640x320 (2:1, `cv.width=640;cv.height=320`), but `.el-canvas-wrap` is `width:100%; height:160px` (440x160 = 2.75:1 at a 440px card, 332x160 on a 390px phone). With the default `object-fit:fill` the art was stretched about 1.4x sideways (the flames smeared wide).

**Fix.** One CSS declaration: `object-fit:contain` on `.el-canvas-wrap canvas`. All of the art is kept and shown at its authored aspect; because the card is dark and the motif has its own dark ground, the unused sides blend with the card. `object-fit:cover` was tried and crops the top and bottom of the art; it was not chosen because it loses content. The module already draws at the screen's density (store 1920x960 at ratio 3), so no resolution change was needed.

**Verified.** `node scripts/verify-elements-canvas-aspect.js`: PASSED 12 of 12 at 1000@1, 1000@3 and 390@3 (nine canvases exist and are sized; displayed art keeps the store aspect; store not below the screen density; no page errors). The same script against the pre-fix `main`: 3 failures, one per configuration (`fit: fill`, 440x160 box on a 2:1 store). `python3 -m unittest scripts.tests.test_elements_canvas_aspect`: OK; the browser case passes with `OMEGA_ELEMENTS_BROWSER=1`. Screenshots of card 1 at 1000px, dpr 2, before / `contain` / `cover` were compared by eye.

**Not done / not verified (and what the sweep ruled out).**
- **Art direction:** `contain` makes the art about 27 percent narrower than before (it fills 320 of 440 px). If the owner prefers a full-width crop, `cover` is a one-word change.
- **Not defects, so not changed:** the 637 "uniform but below the screen density" canvases are 2x stores on a dpr-3 screen: the full-window backdrops `omega-fx` and `omega-atmosphere` (capped at 2x on purpose, GAP_ANALYSIS S), and small icon and ring canvases. `pulse.html` `dom-canvas` and `cipher.html` `cipher-wave` were flagged but had not been drawn at sweep time (the first draws only after data and a real width, the second only after typing, and both refit when they do), so they were left alone. The nav brand canvas `#on-bcv` has a 12x44 box on every page, but it sits under the fixed menu button (`#om-open`) and is not visible.
- **Not measured:** the 929 canvases that were not visible at load (hidden tabs, off-screen). Hidden canvases are where the real bugs have been (the `media` posters were drawn at a made-up size while hidden), so this sweep does not show that they are fine. At dpr 3 only; phone-width and dpr 1 were checked for this page only.
