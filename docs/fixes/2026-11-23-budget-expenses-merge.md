### The budget page merged into expenses (one store, one page), and a phone layout bug fixed on four money pages (2026-11-23)

**Why.** `docs/PAGE_CONSOLIDATION.md` already listed `budget` + `expenses` under "Should merge (strong evidence)", and a previous change had turned `expenses.html` into a view over `budget.html`'s store (`omega_budget_months`, `omega_budget_targets`; `expenses.html` comment: "the same month showed two spending totals, CLAUDE.md 8.1 class 8"). What remained was two UIs over one store: independent code (about 8 percent similar), two places to enter the same money. Requested by the owner ("go ahead with the money cluster"). The rule in that doc is that a merge loses nothing only if the removed page is a subset of the survivor, so the gaps were catalogued first: `expenses.html` showed only the current month, with no month navigation, no yearly chart and no monthly savings-rate chart.

**Fix.** (1) `expenses.html` gains a month navigator and per-month overview on its BUDGET tab (income, expenses, surplus, savings rate, every category's actual against its limit; spending with no limit is shown with a dash) and a TRENDS tab (year buttons, income vs expenses by month, savings rate by month, each canvas carrying a text summary of its numbers). Months are read by store KEY exactly as `budget.html` did (an entry counts in the month it was filed under even if its date says otherwise), and month arithmetic is integer: `budget.html` built the next month with `toISOString()` on a local date, which lands a month early east of UTC. (2) `budget.html` is deleted; `/budget` and `/budget.html` redirect to `/expenses` (301, in `vercel.json`, confirmed in the built `_redirects`). (3) Every reference follows: nav, search (one "BUDGET & EXPENSES" entry that still matches "budget"), dashboard links, the invest hub (two cards became one), the Vault. (4) Registries and baselines: eight page registries edited surgically (rows removed, counts recomputed from rows), hub ownership regenerated, `EVIDENCE_MATRIX.md`, census, README and the index.html surface count (243 to 242), the CSP and XSS ratchet baselines (only this page's entries; I reverted a wider `--update` that would have folded in unrelated drift), `test_omega_domain_hub_workspaces` counts (185 to 184, 200 to 199), and the finance-notice tests (14 pages to 13).

**Found on the way, fixed.** (a) **The Vault was wrong for expenses users**: `omega-vault-core.js` read `omega_expenses` for its EXPENSES row, a key `expenses.html` moves away and deletes, so that row went empty while the BUDGET row read the shared store. It is now one "BUDGET & EXPENSES" row reading the store, plus the legacy list only while it still exists, so a member who has not reopened the page since is not shown as empty. (b) **A stored-markup sink**: the largest-expense insight put the member-entered description into `innerHTML` unescaped, and the page imports backup files; it now goes through `escapeHTML`. (c) **Phone layout on four money pages**: on `expenses.html`, `investment.html`, `revenue.html` and `wallet.html` the page emblem was a direct child of the flex-row shell, i.e. a third column 364 px wide and 3,900 to 4,600 px tall, over the tab row at 375 px (Playwright could not click the BUDGET tab: "data-page-emblem intercepts pointer events"). `budget.html` did NOT have this (its emblem sits inside `main`, 243x235), so redirecting `/budget` to `/expenses` would have made the page unusable on a phone for budget users; the merge was not done until it worked. `omega-page-emblem.js` has a `reseat()` for exactly this and it does not run on these pages (its preconditions all hold in the live DOM, so it loses a race with the stylesheet that makes `.shell` a flex row; not investigated further). Fix: each mount moved inside `<main>` in the markup. Measured before/after at 375 px: emblem 364x4,628 / 4,535 / 4,412 / 3,866 on investment / revenue / wallet / expenses, 227x235 inside `main` after. The other money pages (the `.page-shell` ones are column layouts, 319x235) were measured too and are fine.

**Verified.** (1) Equivalence against the retired page, once, before deleting it: one seeded store (including an expense dated last month but filed under this one, a month spending more than it earned, a month with no income, and last year) loaded in the old `budget.html` through its own UI and in the new `expenses.html` through its own UI: income, expenses, surplus, savings rate and all eight category actuals agreed for four months, and the yearly income/expense and savings-rate series agreed for two years. (2) `node scripts/verify-expenses-merge.js` 38/38 and `scripts/tests/test_budget_expenses_merge.py` (10 static contracts plus the browser run): the expectations are computed in the test from its own seed, not from the old page. Covers the month navigator and overview, the filing trap, year boundaries in three time zones including UTC+14, the yearly series, adding an entry through the real form (it lands in the store in `budget.html`'s shape), a hostile description, legacy-ledger migration, keyboard use, 375 px, the invest hub card and the Vault row (including an un-migrated member). **Mutation-tested, six ways, each caught**: month step via `toISOString()` (fails only in UTC+14, which confirms the original bug), group actuals by entry date (fails the trap), savings rate against expenses (7 failures), budget tab ignoring the selected month, description unescaped, Vault ignoring the legacy list. `verify-finance-notice.js` 120/120 now includes a phone-layout check for every money page, mutation-tested by putting wallet's emblem back (3,849 px column, fails). Gates: contract suite 95 of 95, `xss-sink-ratchet` and `csp-inline-ratchet` pass without raising any baseline (this page's XSS count went 17 to 12).
