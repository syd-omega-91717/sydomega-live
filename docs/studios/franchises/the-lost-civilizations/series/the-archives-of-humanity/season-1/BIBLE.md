## Premise
A modern expedition finds evidence of advanced ancient civilizations that vanished independently and left strikingly similar records. Season 1 follows one
dig, in one desert, to one sealed kingdom, and answers the film's premise on screen: this disappearance was *deliberate*. The season's question is what a
people owes to those who will dig them up.

## Method (so the mystery stays fair)
Every clue is a physical object a viewer can see: a sentence, a tool mark, a salt line in a wall, a wiped shelf. The show does not explain by narration. The
expedition may be wrong in a scene, and when it is, the next scene says so. Nothing is "ancient astronauts": the people were people.

## Reconciliation with the wider canon
The disappearances belong to Age III; the expeditions are modern (registry reconciliation). That keeps this series clear of the twelve-thousand-year Accord
in *Zodiac Wars*. No character here knows of the Houses, the Nexus or the Stones.

## Continuity locks
1. No living member of any lost civilization appears in Season 1.
2. Every inscription shown is a real-looking, internally consistent sentence in an invented script that Mercer reads one word at a time; the audience learns words in order.
3. The kingdom left *on purpose*; the reason is withheld until episode 107.
4. The Archivist is a pronoun-free presence: no face, no voice, evidence only.
5. Surname Ardent is also used in *The Last Galactic Empire* (Juno Ardent). Recorded as conflict C-26: no relation unless the Convergence earns it.

## Episode guide (8)
- 101 **The Same Sentence** — a sealed chamber holds a line Mercer has seen in two other lost cultures.
- 102 **Salt Line** — a wall records a flood that never happened here.
- 103 **The Quiet Dig** — a rival team arrives with permits and a different plan.
- 104 **Nefara's Count** — the Queen's records list her people by number, then stop.
- 105 **The Stair** — Samir goes below the plan; the cost is a week.
- 106 **What They Took** — what the city removed tells more than what it left.
- 107 **The Reason** — why a kingdom would choose to be lost.
- 108 **The Last Shelf** — the wiped shelf's missing object is found; the Archivist's hand is shown only as work.
