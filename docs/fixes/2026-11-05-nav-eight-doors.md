The dock, its tooltips and the phone drawer render from `GROUPS`; the highlighted door follows `ACTIVE_GROUP`. Nothing else changed: `PS`, `SECTIONS`, `OmegaAxis`, the full launcher, search and the owner deck still use the 15 sections, so every page keeps its accent, hero and URL, and no page was removed.
