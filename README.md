## ⌖&nbsp;&nbsp;What This Is

Most people run their life through a dozen disconnected apps — one for
habits, one for money, one for learning, one for the people they care
about. Each holds a fragment. None of them holds the person.

**SYD OMEGA 91717 exists to end that fragmentation.** It is a single,
private realm where the things that actually compose a life — discipline,
knowledge, wealth, lineage, creation, and community — live under one
roof, one identity, and one design language.

**Membership is invitation-only.** Every request is reviewed by hand.
Entry is not purchased and never auto-granted. Once approved, every member
receives their own zodiac sign, element, and guiding persona — a
coordinate that is genuinely theirs, assigned by the platform itself, never
chosen.

It is not a productivity app with a theme on top. The mythology *is* the
architecture: signs, elements, and guiding personas are the navigation
system, the progress model, and the vocabulary all at once. Learn your
sign and your element once, and you already know your way through every
realm.

## ✧&nbsp;&nbsp;What It Represents

Three convictions shape everything here.

**A life is one system, not twelve dashboards.** Progress in discipline
should visibly move standing in the wider realm. Knowledge earned should
compound into authority. What you build should be inheritable. The
platform is designed so these are literally connected, not merely
adjacent.

**Meaning should be structural.** Every member receives a sign, an
element, and a guiding persona at genesis — assigned, never chosen, and
evolved only through what you actually do. Twelve signs, nine elements,
and a lattice of positions give each member a coordinate that is genuinely
theirs, and a language shared with everyone else inside.

**Sovereignty is literal, not marketing.** *Sovereign* here describes who
holds the keys. The realm is governed by a single Architect. Entry is by
invitation and reviewed by hand. And your data, once inside, is yours —
to inspect, to export, or to erase entirely, whenever you decide.

## ◎&nbsp;&nbsp;How Standing Works

Nothing here is a vanity number. Standing is built from three axes that
correspond to genuinely different kinds of effort:

<table>
<tr><td align="center" width="120"><b>Axis A</b><br><sub>Knowledge</sub></td><td>What you have learned and can demonstrate — study, reading, examination, and mastery of the platform's canon and your own skill.</td></tr>
<tr><td align="center"><b>Axis B</b><br><sub>Mastery</sub></td><td>What you have practiced until it holds — discipline sustained over time, daily habits, consistency, not intensity in bursts.</td></tr>
<tr><td align="center"><b>Axis C</b><br><sub>Contribution</sub></td><td>What you have given to the realm and the people in it — support, creation, sharing, community building.</td></tr>
</table>

**The Authority Score:** The three axes combine into a single authority
score. As it rises, you unlock **Gates** — thresholds that mark genuine
changes in what you can reach and do. Tiers range from 1–9, with each
tier unlocking new realms, features, and capabilities.

Gates are not purchases and cannot be skipped. Progression is deliberately
slow at the edges and impossible to shortcut: your standing reflects
genuine effort over time, and a standing that can be bought is not a
standing.

**Inheritance & Legacy:** Your character evolves alongside your standing.
Traits, achievements, and authority pass to your heirs, merge into the
bloodline, and carry the Order across generations — making your progress
tangible beyond your own lifetime.

## Ω&nbsp;&nbsp;Your Privacy, Your Sovereignty

Your data belongs to you. That is the whole point of the word "sovereignty."

**What you control:**

- **See everything.** The Privacy Centre shows exactly what data the platform holds about you: your profile, standing, every action, every note, every connection.
- **Adjust consent.** You control what telemetry and features are active. Turn off tracking, analytics, or communication features anytime.
- **Export your life.** Download your complete account data in a standard format. Everything you've built, learned, created, and contributed is yours to take.
- **Erase on demand.** Request permanent deletion of your account and all its contents. No queue, no waiting for support — the button is in the app.

**How we respect it:**

- **Closed to search engines.** The realm is not indexed by Google, Bing, or any public search engine.
- **Closed to the public.** What happens inside stays between the members and the Architect. No data is sold, rented, or shared with third parties.
- **End-to-end is optional, not forced.** Sensitive data can be encrypted if you choose, and the keys are yours alone.
- **Audit trails for you.** Every access to your data is logged and visible to you. If someone else views your profile, you know.

Full legal terms are available in-app under **Privacy** and **Terms**. They
are written in plain language, not legalese, because this is your data and you
deserve to understand what we do with it.

The visual language is deliberately cinematic and futuristic, but the underlying product model is **evidence-first and governance-driven**.

## Ω The Core Principle

Omega is not intended to become a giant collection of independent pages.

Its current architecture defines a **ONE_GOVERNED_CORE_MANY_DOMAIN_PROJECTIONS** operating model.

### The shared core

| Core | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Identity & policy | Authentication, authorization, roles, ownership boundaries |
| Page contracts | What every page is for, what it reads, what it can do, and what evidence it requires |
| Capability registry | Canonical declaration of platform capabilities |
| Task fabric | User work from request → authorization → execution → verification |
| Data & content fabric | Canonical content, provenance, rights, lifecycle, and access policy |
| Event & evidence fabric | Durable events, evidence, lineage, replay, and auditability |
| Truth vocabulary | Clear distinction between live, calculated, simulated, lore, unavailable, blocked, and other states |
| Navigation & design | Shared information architecture and visual primitives |
| Release evidence | Production gates that distinguish source code from provider/runtime proof |

The governing flow is:

**SOURCE REQUIREMENT → DOMAIN → PAGE → CAPABILITY → TASK → AUTHORIZATION → EXECUTION → EVENT → EVIDENCE → TRUTH STATE → RELEASE STATE**

This prevents a visually complete feature from being mistaken for a production-complete feature.

AI features are expected to:

4. Distinguish generated or inferred information from verified facts.

## ◇ Truth Is a First-Class Product Feature

Omega deliberately avoids presenting every number, card, achievement, balance, or status as real merely because a UI can render it.

The platform's unified truth vocabulary includes states such as:

**LIVE · IMPLEMENTED · RUNTIME-VERIFIED · PRODUCTION-VERIFIED · PARTIAL · CALCULATED · SIMULATED · USER-CREATED · EMPTY · UNAVAILABLE · UNVERIFIED · BLOCKED · LORE · FUTURE · REJECTED · REPLACED**

This matters for a platform that combines real-world services with simulation, narrative, visualization, AI, and game-like progression.

### Example

A simulated market scenario can be valuable. It must simply be presented as **SIMULATED**, not as a live financial result.

A calculated progression score can be useful. It must not be represented as provider-verified authority unless the required evidence exists.

This distinction is part of the architecture, not merely a UI convention.

## ▲ Evidence-First Engineering

Omega uses an explicit production-readiness ladder:

**SPECIFIED → IMPLEMENTED → CONNECTED → PERSISTED → SECURED → TESTED → DEPLOYED → LIVE-VERIFIED**

A source file, SQL migration, UI component, or successful local test does not by itself prove provider-side production behavior.

The repository therefore maintains release evidence and production gates covering areas including:

- Supabase security
- RLS and database performance
- RPC privilege boundaries
- Migration reproducibility
- Payment lifecycle
- Disaster recovery
- Privacy lifecycle
- Provider credentials
- Scale and capacity
- AI quality
- Vercel production deployment
- Owner security

Some of these gates are intentionally **PENDING**, **PARTIAL**, or **BLOCKED** until provider-side evidence exists.

That is deliberate.

**Omega does not use a green-looking README to hide a red production dependency.**

## 🛠 Development Principles

Before adding a feature, answer:

1. **What user problem does this solve?**
2. **Which domain owns it?**
3. **Which canonical capability does it use?**
4. **Which authoritative data source does it require?**
5. **Who is allowed to perform the action?**
6. **What happens for unauthorized users?**
7. **What is the truth state?**
8. **What events and evidence are required?**
9. **How does it fail and retry?**
10. **How is it tested?**
11. **What proves it works in the real provider environment?**
12. **How is it rolled back or retired?**
13. **Who owns its operational cost and residual risk?**

### Definition of done

A feature is not considered production-complete merely because:

- its page renders;
- its JavaScript exists;
- a SQL migration exists;
- a prompt exists;
- a static test passes; or
- a UI control appears clickable.

Production completion requires the applicable contract, authorization cases, negative cases, failure handling, traceability, tests, runtime evidence, deployment evidence, and rollback path.

## ◉ Privacy & User Control

Omega is designed around the principle that users should be able to understand and control their data.

The platform architecture therefore treats privacy as a lifecycle:

**COLLECT → USE → PROTECT → DISCLOSE → RETAIN → EXPORT → ERASE**

The implementation and provider evidence for each stage must be verified independently.

Do not interpret the word **sovereign** as a legal exemption from privacy, financial, security, intellectual-property, accessibility, or other applicable obligations.
