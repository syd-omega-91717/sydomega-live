## 9. Explicit conflict classes

Historical concepts requiring classification before implementation include:

### A. Sovereignty and legal immunity
Treat as lore or product narrative unless lawful evidence establishes otherwise. They must never create system permissions.

### B. DNA / biological authorization
Do not implement as an authorization mechanism. Real biometric/health capabilities require explicit consent, security, lawful purpose, retention/deletion controls and specialist review.

### C. Psychological / mental-state control
Do not infer sensitive traits for eligibility or material decisions. User-declared preferences and boundaries are safer than hidden psychological profiling.

### D. Offensive cyber-warfare
Translate into defensive security engineering: detection, isolation, revocation, rate limiting, containment, incident response and audited automation.

### E. Physics and impossible performance claims
Translate only testable objectives into SLOs, benchmarks and acceptance criteria.

### F. Extraterrestrial ownership/control
Keep as simulation/lore unless independently verified lawful real-world capability exists.

### G. Financial and token-economy rules
Historical formulas are not financial truth. Production finance requires authoritative state, authorization, idempotency, reconciliation, auditability and legal/compliance review.

### H. Historical credentials
Treat credential-looking values as exposed. They must never be reused. Provider-side revocation/rotation is required where applicable.
