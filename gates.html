GATES SCIENCE
01
THRESHOLD THEORY
Cognitive thresholds are qualitative jumps in capability — not linear accumulation. Crossing a gate represents a phase transition: new perception, new capacity, new level of performance that cannot be undone.
02
THE HERO'S JOURNEY
Campbell's monomyth maps 12 stages of transformation matching the 12 gates: call, threshold crossing, trials, ordeal, reward, return. Each gate is a trial the hero must overcome to evolve.
03
ZONE OF PROXIMAL DEVELOPMENT
Vygotsky's ZPD — the gap between what you can do alone and what you can do with guidance — is the exact zone where gate attempts are most productive. Too easy = no growth. Too hard = no progress.
04
MASTERY-BASED PROGRESSION
Gates enforce mastery before advancement — proven more effective than time-based progression. Students who master prerequisites perform 80%+ better on advanced material (Bloom, 1984).
05
CHUNKING & EXPERTISE
Chess masters hold 50,000+ board positions as single chunks in long-term memory. Gates measure whether you can access domain chunks fluently — the hallmark of genuine expertise over surface knowledge.
06
GROWTH MINDSET
Dweck's research: viewing gates as challenges to overcome (not fixed tests of ability) increases pass rates. The fail path is data, not judgment — it maps exactly what to practice next.
