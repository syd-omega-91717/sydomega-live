LEDGER ARCHITECTURE
SOVEREIGN ASSET REGISTER
The Asset Ledger is a record of all sovereign asset classes within the SYD OMEGA 91717 platform. Assets span three categories: Sovereign (core platform infrastructure), Digital (data and blockchain), and Physical (tangible reserve assets).
THE 104,976-NODE LATTICE
The full lattice is 12 × 12 × 9 × 9 × 9 = 104,976 coordinate nodes. Each node encodes a unique combination of matrix track, phase, and axis values (A, B, C). Your lattice position is your sovereign coordinate.
AUTHORITY FORMULA
AUTH = √(A³+B³+C³) × φ/e where φ = 1.6180339887 (golden ratio) and e = 2.7182818285 (Euler's number). Maximum authority of 27.8367 is achieved at axis values (9,9,9). All ledger valuations are anchored to AUTH.
ASSET STATUS CLASSES
Assets carry four status types: VERIFIED (cryptographically confirmed active assets), ACTIVE (operationally live), RESONANCE LOCK (gate-locked pending authority threshold), PENDING (staged for future activation on compliance sign-off).
