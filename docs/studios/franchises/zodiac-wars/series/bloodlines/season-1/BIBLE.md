# ZODIAC WARS: BLOODLINES — Season 1: The Soul Door (Bible, Draft 1, PROPOSED)

**Status: SEASON BIBLE DRAFT 1.** Not approved, not produced. A bible, eight beat sheets, and a short first draft of episode 101
(`episodes/E01_THE_NEXT_COUNT.fountain`). Written under the founder's delegation on 2026-11-07 ("start another franchise"), after
`The Rise of Aries` (screenplay draft 1, itself **not** founder-approved) and the Zodiac Wars canon draft
(`docs/canon/zodiac-wars/BIBLE.md`, whose own header says everything after the founder's pitch is the assistant's proposal).
**Everything here is therefore a proposal on top of a proposal.** Format: 8 episodes of about 50 minutes. Registry ids:
`zodiac-wars`, series title `Zodiac Wars: Bloodlines`.

## The structural decisions (read this first)

1. **Series shape (conflict C-24).** The registry says *Bloodlines* is 4 seasons of 8 episodes. The canon draft and the Rise of
   Aries package instead describe a ten-episode limited series ("Season 1 opens on the Soul door") and a five-season series.
   **Decision: keep the registry's 4 x 8.** The canon draft gives the Nexus four forgotten guardians (Soul, Space, Void, The All) and
   there are twelve Houses, so the arithmetic is exact: **one guardian and three Houses per season.** Season 1 is Soul and the three
   Houses the film already put in play: Aries, Libra and Pisces. The ten-episode limited series and a fifth, Null season are *not*
   adopted; whether the Null gets a season is the founder's call. (The live micro-series *The Twelve Houses* is a separate product.)
2. **Season 1 starts exactly where the film stops.** The film's last spoken line is Soul's: "You counted well. Now count the next."
   Season 1 answers *what* the next count is.
3. **The next count is the Accord's own cost.** Ossara's forty thousand were the cost of *his* plan, and Kael made him say them.
   Nobody ever said the cost of the **Solar Accord**. The Accord bought twelve thousand years of peace; the canon draft says the first
   war "was not won: it was paused". The dead of that war were never counted, and Soul is the guardian of what is carried across
   a death. An uncarried dead is what the Null feeds on (canon draft section 4: the Null is what an unheld system decays into).
   **The film already points at this.** Adaeze tells the Foreman and the Warden that clause nine exists because "twelve thousand years ago
   somebody died of thirst on a world that had water they weren't allowed to touch". The reason for **one** clause is remembered. The
   season's premise is that every clause exists because someone died, and the Court remembers exactly one.
4. **Episodes are named for the first eight gates** (`INITIATE`, `ACOLYTE`, `SCHOLAR`, `KEEPER`, `GUARDIAN`, `ARCHITECT`, `SOVEREIGN`,
   `VANGUARD`), the platform's own gate names (`omega-canon.json`, `authority.gate_names`), in order. `PRIME` and `APEX` stay held
   back for the finale of the whole series, as the canon draft says.
5. **"Bloodline" means what a House carries across a death:** its dead and its debts, not a family tree. Each of the three Houses
   inherited one unpaid cost from its founding regent, and each regent is one of the registry's proposed Age IV regents (Ares
   Valorian, Lucian Balance, Phaedra Mir).

## Premise and tone

Weeks after the Aries bell is rung, a forgotten guardian's door stands a hand's width open. From far away only one nineteen-year-old can hear it; inside the vault anyone can.
It asks him to count a cost the peace was built on. Tone: the film's, which is plain, civic and unshowy; a court, a foundry and an archive,
long conversations about what a promise costs, one great set piece per episode. The season's question: **what does a line owe its
dead, and who may carry them?**

## Recurring motifs

- **"Say the cost."** The foundry oath, and the series' method. Said once per episode, by a different person, in a different mouth.
- **The bell.** The new Aries bell has Orla Tevane's name cast on its lip. It is rung once at the end of the season, not before.
- **9.1717 Hz.** The Nexus hum, exactly that frequency (registry C-07). Never visualised as a flicker.
- **"Hold the other end."** Solenne's phrase in the film (registry C-18, a cross-franchise echo with *The Seven Stones*). Used once, in
  episode 103, and not again this season.
- **The Unbled's blood.** The film's post-credit scene: the Aries Shadow bleeds for the first time. In this season its wound opens a
  little more each time something uncounted is counted.

## Continuity locks with the film (a season that breaks one is wrong)

1. **Soul's door stands a hand's width open and has not opened further.** Soul has shown only a hand and spoken to Kael; it opens fully
   only in episode 108.
2. **Five rings are lit and four are dark; the dark ones have stopped but not lit.** Only Soul's door has moved.
3. **Ossara is in the Court's custody and said "I'll stand trial."** Adaeze answered "You'll be heard. By anyone who wants to."
4. **The Unbled has bled once** (the film's post-credit scene). Ares's only comment so far is "Interesting."
5. **The Null said "I will remember you" and has not spoken since.** It does not speak in Season 1 until the finale, and then only once.
6. **Orla Tevane is dead and her name is on the bell.** The film's last foundry scene is "weeks later" and the bell has been rung once.
7. **Ages:** Kael 19, Adaeze 34, Solenne 73, Ima 8 at the start; the season spans about eight weeks.
8. **Knights are archetypes, not members** (platform canon, `factions.html`): Aries counts the cost aloud, Libra changes the law, Pisces
   tells the old story to a child.
9. **The Light rulers are voices only**, as in the film: no body, no face, no appearance in a room.
10. **Orla's oath is "say the cost before the pour"** and Kael's practice is to say it before every act of consequence.
11. **Sentinel** is the foundry perimeter and Kael's ship, a voice with no body.
12. **The frequency is 9.1717 Hz** everywhere.

## Episode guide

### 101. INITIATE — "The Next Count"
*Cold open:* the Nexus at night. Soul's hand taps the white door and Soul counts under its breath: one bell, one tongue, one master.
*Logline:* Kael learns what "the next" is, and finds a page of the Accord that was never filled in.
- A: the Foundry, weeks on; Kael cannot sleep; he walks to the casting pit every night and hears the door.
- B: Meridian; Adaeze shows him the Accord's original schedule of clauses: only clause nine has its reason written (the death by thirst); every other reason is blank.
- C: Soul explains what is carried across a death, and what happens to what is not.
*Cliffhanger:* a drop of red falls on the first blank reason.

### 102. ACOLYTE — "The Hearing"
*Logline:* Ossara is heard, and what he knows about counting changes what the Court thinks it is for. (Libra.)
- A: the Hall of the Seats; the open hearing; anyone may speak.
- B: Hera's voice and the Sworn's lingering letter-of-the-law reflex.
- C: Kael and Adaeze's argument about whether a law can carry the dead.
*Cliffhanger:* Ossara asks to be given a first name from the blank page.

### 103. SCHOLAR — "The Old Story" (Pisces)
- Solenne and Ima in the Deep Archive; the Drowned's silence; the one use of "hold the other end".
*Cliffhanger:* Ima finds a shelf with no labels.

### 104. KEEPER — "What the Foundry Kept" (Aries)
- Orla's notebook; the foundry's old ledgers; Tamsin and Brannock find the first Aries cost.
*Cliffhanger:* the notebook's last page is the Accord's blank page, in Orla's hand.

### 105. GUARDIAN — "The Regents"
- Flashback to the signing: Ares Valorian, Lucian Balance and Phaedra Mir, and what each decided not to count.
*Cliffhanger:* the fourth signature, which none of the Houses remember.

### 106. ARCHITECT — "The Law of the Carried" (Libra)
- Adaeze drafts the amendment; the Houses push back; the vote.
*Cliffhanger:* the law passes and Soul's door moves.

### 107. SOVEREIGN — "The Unbled" (Aries)
- The Shadow's wound; Ossara and Kael; what a Shadow does when it begins to count.
*Cliffhanger:* the Unbled counts its first name aloud.

### 108. VANGUARD — "The Soul Door"
- The door opens; the three Knights carry the first names; the Null speaks once; the next door (Space) stirs.
*Cliffhanger:* the season ends on the Space door.
