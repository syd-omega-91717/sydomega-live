AWARD ARCHITECTURE
AXIS-GATED AWARDS
Most awards are gated on the authority score and its component axes. The Lorekeeper, Grand Master, and Builder awards each require a single axis at 9.0 — maximum depth on one dimension. The Apex Sovereign award requires all three simultaneously, which is the rarest outcome: AUTH = 27.8367.
PIONEER SCARCITY
Twelve Pioneer badges exist — one per zodiac sign. The first member of each sign to reach Gate VI claims that sign's badge permanently. No member can claim more than one Pioneer badge, and once claimed it cannot be transferred. The badges record the Order's founding hierarchy.
ANNUAL SNAPSHOT
The Sovereign Seal is awarded based on the December 31 authority snapshot — not a rolling leaderboard. This means a member can peak at the right moment and claim the award even without the permanent highest authority. The record is stored in the Sovereign Ledger permanently.
EXTERNAL REGISTRY
External awards submitted by members are reviewed by the platform owner before registration. They do not alter axis scores but appear as verified credentials in the member codex. The categories (academic, industry, creative, athletic, civic) align with the five sovereign contribution domains.
