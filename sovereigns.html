LEADERBOARD ARCHITECTURE
PRIVACY BY DESIGN
No member name, email, or personal identifier is shown in the Hall of Sovereigns. Members are identified only by their rank number and whether "YOU" indicates their own row. The privacy protocol is enforced client-side by substituting identity with rank ordinal before rendering.
AUTHORITY RANKING
Ranking is by authority score: AUTH = sqrt(A³ + B³ + C³) × φ/e. The leaderboard fetches approved profiles ordered by axis_a (as a proxy), then computes authority client-side and re-sorts the result. This avoids a server-side sort on a computed column while keeping the ranking accurate.
MATERIAL TIERS
The twelve Material Tiers — Sand through Omega Master — map to the floor of the minimum axis score. A member at A=6, B=4, C=7 has a minimum of 4, placing them at Steel. The material tier is a visual shorthand for overall balance, not just total authority. Imbalanced members have lower material tiers than their total authority would imply.
THRONE DISPLAY
The Apex Throne highlights the top three sovereigns with special visual treatment: gold border for rank 1, silver for rank 2, bronze for rank 3. The throne panel is a separate view to give the top three appropriate ceremony without overwhelming the main leaderboard table.
